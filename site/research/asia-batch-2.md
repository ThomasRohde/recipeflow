## Batata Vada

- Source: [Sanjeev Kapoor — Batata Vada](https://www.sanjeevkapoor.com/Recipe/Batata-Vada.html)
- Editorial descriptor: Mumbai potato balls in a gram-flour coat, with a vividly green coriander-chilli masala and mustard tempering under the crust.
- Yield and timing: serves 4; 21–25 minutes preparation; 11–15 minutes cooking.
- Displayed ingredient evidence: 4 medium boiled and mashed potatoes; 2 cups gram flour; 1 cup chopped fresh coriander; 4–6 garlic cloves; 3 green chillies; 1/2-inch ginger; 1 teaspoon mustard seeds; 1/2 teaspoon asafoetida; 4–6 curry leaves; 1/2 teaspoon turmeric; salt to taste.
- Method evidence: grind coriander, garlic, chilli, ginger, and a little water to a fine paste; splutter mustard in 1 tablespoon oil; add asafoetida, curry leaves, paste, and turmeric and cook 2 minutes; add potatoes and salt, sprinkle with water, add coriander, cover and cook 2–3 minutes; cool 10–15 minutes; whisk a semi-thick lump-free besan batter with salt, red chilli, turmeric, coriander powder, cumin powder, asafoetida, and sufficient water; add baking soda and rest 10 minutes; heat sufficient oil; form and lightly press balls; batter and deep-fry over medium heat for 4–5 minutes until golden and crisp; drain and serve hot.
- Exact ambiguities: the displayed ingredient list omits both oils, all three water uses, red chilli powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and baking soda even though the method names them. The listed coriander, salt, turmeric, and asafoetida are each used twice without allocation. No vada count or oil temperature is stated. Pav is mentioned only as an introductory serving idea and is not modeled as required.

## Aloo Tikki

- Source: [NDTV Food — Aloo Tikki Recipe](https://food.ndtv.com/recipe-aloo-tikki-231373)
- Editorial descriptor: Chilled potato-and-pea street-food patties with freshly roasted spices, ginger, browned onion, green chilli, and coriander.
- Source choice: Sanjeev Kapoor did not expose a complete exact plain Aloo Tikki recipe in accessible search results; its accessible Aloo Til Tikki page lists only potatoes and sesame while the method names several additional ingredients. NDTV Food's exact plain Aloo Tikki page supplies the complete ingredient section and all numbered steps.
- Yield and timing: serves 6; 10 minutes preparation; 45 minutes cooking; 55 minutes total.
- Ingredient evidence: 4 peeled boiled potatoes; 1/2 teaspoon each coriander seeds, pepper, and cumin; 1/2 cup boiled peas; sea salt to taste; 2 teaspoons ginger paste; 100 ml refined oil; 1 julienned onion; 1 chopped green chilli; 1 small bunch fresh coriander; 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder.
- Method evidence: dry-roast coriander, pepper, and cumin and powder them; mash potatoes and peas separately, then combine; add ginger; sauté onion in oil and add it; add salt, roasted spice, chilli, coriander, red chilli, and 1/2 teaspoon oil; form balls and flatten; refrigerate 30 minutes; sprinkle with flour; shallow-fry in oil; garnish and serve hot.
- Exact ambiguities: the overview calls the patties deep-fried while the numbered method says shallow-fry; the YAML follows the method. The 100 ml oil is shared among onion sautéing, the exact 1/2-teaspoon moisture addition, and frying without a full allocation. Flour appears only in the method and has no type or quantity. The garnish is unnamed. No patty count, frying time, temperature, or sensory endpoint is supplied.

## Perkedel Kentang

- Source: [ResepKoki — Resep Perkedel Kentang](https://resepkoki.id/resep/resep-perkedel-kentang/)
- Source credit: Atsarina Luthfiyyah, Senior Editor, shown on the recipe page; Indonesian method translated faithfully into English for RecipeFlow.
- Editorial descriptor: Indonesian potato-and-corned-beef patties, bound with yolk and flour, protected by an egg-white coat, and fried yellow-brown.
- Yield and timing: 25 pieces; 30 minutes; easy.
- Ingredient evidence: 500 g washed potatoes; 100 g corned beef; 2 thinly sliced celery stalks; 3 tablespoons wheat flour; 1 lightly beaten egg yolk; 1 egg white for coating; cooking oil as needed; water as needed; under `bumbu halus`, 3 shallots, 2 garlic cloves, 1/2 teaspoon pepper, and 2 teaspoons salt.
- Method evidence: steam potatoes until cooked and tender; peel and mash while warm; sauté the ground seasoning until fragrant, add corned beef, and cook until it changes colour; mix with potato, celery, yolk, and flour; shape all into flattened rounds; heat plenty of oil over medium; dip in egg white and fry until yellow-brown; lift, drain, and serve.
- Exact ambiguities: the `bumbu halus` heading implies a ground seasoning, but the numbered steps omit grinding instructions. Water is listed but never used. The as-needed oil is not allocated between sautéing and frying. The recipe tips permit increasing flour to taste and optionally refrigerating shaped patties for 30 minutes, but neither change is required by the numbered method.
