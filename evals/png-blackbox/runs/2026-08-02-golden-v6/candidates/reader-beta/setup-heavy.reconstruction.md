# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- 2 lemons, finely grated for zest and juiced; strain the juice
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g softened unsalted butter
- Additional butter and sugar for coating the ramekins; the image does not give amounts

## Equipment and advance setup

- Six 180 mL ramekins
- A heavy baking sheet
- A perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk
- A folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer

1. Put the empty heavy baking sheet on the center oven rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Brush the six ramekins upward with butter, then coat the entire inside of each with sugar.
3. Chill the clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes.
4. Arrange the folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven as a landing station.
5. Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved there without delay or obstruction.

## Method

1. Cook the lemon zest and strained juice with the milk and egg yolks to make a thick, warm lemon custard base. The image does not specify the cooking temperature or time.
2. In the chilled bowl, whip the egg whites with the superfine sugar until glossy peaks stand upright but their tips still bend.
3. Gently fold the lemon custard base, glossy soft-peak meringue, and softened butter together to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture. The image does not specify the exact folding order.
4. Divide the mixture evenly among the prepared ramekins. Level the tops and clean the rims.
5. Transfer the ramekins to the hot baking sheet and bake at 200 °C for 12 to 14 minutes.
6. Serve the six tall lemon soufflés immediately.
