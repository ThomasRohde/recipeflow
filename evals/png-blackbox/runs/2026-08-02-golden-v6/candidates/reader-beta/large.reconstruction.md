# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt
- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- 3 limes, zest and juice
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds

## Advance setup

1. Heat a wide, heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately. Keep it ready for toasting the spices, browning the chicken in batches, and toasting the almonds.
2. Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat for the sauce branch.
3. Rinse the basmati rice until the water runs clear, soak it for 30 minutes, then drain it thoroughly when needed.
4. Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls for serving.

## Method

Several parts of this recipe can be prepared as parallel branches.

### Marinate and brown the chicken

1. Toast the coriander and cumin seeds in the hot skillet for 2 to 3 minutes, until fragrant.
2. Combine the chicken thigh pieces with the toasted spices, fine sea salt, and thick yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes.
3. Brown the marinated chicken in batches in 45 mL of the neutral cooking oil for 12 to 16 minutes total, producing deeply browned chicken pieces. The image does not state whether this time is per batch or overall.

### Make the tomato and aromatic sauce

1. Combine the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
2. Sauté this aromatic mixture in the remaining 30 mL neutral cooking oil for 10 to 12 minutes, until it forms a soft, golden aromatic base.
3. Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind a spoon.
4. Add the browned chicken pieces to the concentrated tomato and aromatic sauce. Braise over low heat for 25 to 35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.

### Steam the rice

1. Drain the soaked rice thoroughly.
2. Steam the rice with the filtered water over low heat for 14 to 18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom.

### Make the herb sauce and almonds

1. Blend the cilantro leaves and tender stems with the lime zest and juice until the sauce is vivid green and pourable while retaining tiny visible herb flecks.
2. Toast the sliced almonds in the hot skillet for 3 to 5 minutes, until evenly golden.

### Layer and serve

Layer the steamed basmati rice and tender spiced chicken braise on the warm platter. Finish with ribbons of the cilantro-lime sauce and the toasted almonds. Arrange it so the rice forms an even base, the chicken remains distinct, the herb sauce is distributed in ribbons, and the almonds stay crisp on top. Serve in the warm bowls.
