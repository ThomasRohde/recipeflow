# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Yield: 6 glasses

## Ingredients

- 300 mL cold heavy cream, divided into 250 mL for the mousse and 50 mL for the final rosettes
- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Method

1. Measure 250 mL of the cold cream for the mousse base and reserve the remaining 50 mL for the final rosettes.
2. Melt the dark chocolate until smooth. The image does not specify the melting method or whether the chocolate should cool before it is folded into the cream.
3. Whip the 250 mL cream with the superfine sugar, then fold in the smooth melted chocolate until the mixture holds soft peaks and has no visible chocolate streaks.
4. Portion the airy dark chocolate mousse among six glasses.
