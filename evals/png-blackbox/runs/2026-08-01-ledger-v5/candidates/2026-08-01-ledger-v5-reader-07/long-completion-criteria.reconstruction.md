# Slow Caramel with Explicit Completion Criteria

Makes 450 mL sauce.

## Ingredients

- 300 g granulated sugar
- 90 mL filtered water
- 60 g unsalted butter, cut into 6 pieces
- 240 mL warm heavy cream
- 4 g flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Combine the sugar and water over medium heat and dissolve the sugar without stirring, about 4 to 6 minutes. Continue until every visible crystal has disappeared from both the syrup and the inner wall of the pan, and the liquid looks completely transparent against a white spatula.
2. Continue cooking over medium heat, swirling occasionally, for 8 to 12 minutes. Stop when the syrup is an even deep amber from edge to center, gives off its first faint thread of smoke, and has no dark, bitter spots.
3. Carefully whisk in the butter, warm heavy cream, and flaky sea salt over low heat for 3 to 5 minutes. Continue until the bubbling has subsided, every piece of butter is incorporated, and a spoonful cools to a thick ribbon that remains smooth rather than grainy.

The result is a smooth salted caramel sauce with a deep amber finish.
