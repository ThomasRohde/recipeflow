# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- 2 lemons, finely grated zest and strained juice
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g softened unsalted butter

## Set up

- Chill a perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes before whipping the meringue.
- Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved without delay or obstruction.
- Place a folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven.
- Put an empty heavy baking sheet on the center oven rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.
- Arrange six clean 180 mL ramekins beside the oven.

## Method

1. Cook the finely grated lemon zest and strained juice, whole milk, and egg yolks to make a thick, warm lemon custard base. No time, temperature, or more detailed cooking method is specified.
2. Using the chilled bowl and whisk, whip the superfine sugar and egg whites until the meringue is glossy and the peaks stand upright while their tips still bend.
3. Gently fold the softened butter, all of the warm lemon custard base, and all of the meringue together to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture.
4. Divide all of the mixture evenly among the six ramekins, level the tops, and clean the rims.
5. Transfer the ramekins to the preheated baking sheet and bake at 200 °C for 12 to 14 minutes. Serve the six tall lemon soufflés immediately.
