# Espresso Brownies

Makes 16 brownies.

## Ingredients

- 115 g unsalted butter
- 60 mL freshly brewed strong espresso
- 200 g granulated sugar
- 2.5 mL vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs
- 1.3 g baking soda
- 80 g Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 80 g all-purpose flour
- 1.5 g fine sea salt
- Butter and flour for the pan; amounts are not specified

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 170 °C. Butter and flour an 8 × 8 inch baking pan.
2. Melt the unsalted butter.
3. Mix the freshly brewed strong espresso, all of the melted butter, the granulated sugar, and the vanilla extract until the mixture is glossy.
4. Whisk the eggs with all of the glossy espresso mixture to make an aerated wet batter.
5. Fold the baking soda, Dutch-process cocoa powder, all-purpose flour, and fine sea salt into all of the aerated wet batter to make the espresso brownie batter.
6. Transfer all of the batter to the prepared pan. Bake at 170 °C for 30 to 40 minutes, until a tester emerges with moist crumbs but no raw batter.
