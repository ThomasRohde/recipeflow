# Caramelized Pear and Hazelnut Celebration Tart with Brown-Butter Custard

Makes one 28 cm tart, serving 10 to 12 people.

## Ingredients

- 180 g unsalted cultured butter, cut into small equal cubes
- 6 large Conference pears, ripe but still firm, peeled, cored, and cut lengthwise into evenly sized eighths
- 240 mL heavy cream with at least 35% milk fat
- 4 large free-range eggs, at room temperature
- 175 g skinned roasted hazelnuts, with no bitter papery fragments remaining, ground very finely but not processed into nut butter
- 220 g superfine golden caster sugar
- One 28 cm all-butter sweet pastry shell, blind-baked until deeply golden at the edges and completely cooled in its fluted tart pan

## Before starting

Position an oven rack in the lower third. Preheat the oven, without fan assistance, to 175 °C. After the thermostat first signals readiness, allow at least 20 minutes for the baking stone and oven walls to reach an even temperature.

Arrange the cooled pastry shell on a rimmed tray beside a heatproof landing area. Use a stable work surface protected from hot caramel and splashing custard.

## Method

1. Brown the butter slowly for 8 to 12 minutes, until the milk solids are a deep hazelnut brown and smell nutty, with no black specks.
2. In a separate preparation branch, caramelize every surface of the pear wedges for 12 to 15 minutes. Continue until each wedge is amber on both broad sides but remains firm enough to lift intact. The image does not specify the pan, heat level, or any additional caramelizing ingredient.
3. Whisk all of the brown butter, heavy cream, eggs, finely ground roasted hazelnuts, and superfine golden caster sugar together. Continue until no dry pockets remain and a ribbon drawn across the surface disappears slowly, producing a pourable custard.
4. Arrange all of the caramelized pear wedges in a visible fan in the cooled pastry shell. Pour in all of the custard so it reaches every gap without overflowing the pastry rim.
5. Carefully transfer the tart, on its rimmed tray, to the oven. Bake at 175 °C for 35 to 45 minutes, until the outer custard is set while the center retains a small, coherent wobble when the tray is nudged.
