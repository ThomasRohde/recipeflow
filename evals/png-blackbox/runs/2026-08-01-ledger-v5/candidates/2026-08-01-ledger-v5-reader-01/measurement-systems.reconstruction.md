# Dual-System Buttermilk Biscuits

Makes 10 biscuits.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon (12 g) baking powder
- 1 teaspoon (6 g) fine salt
- 6 tablespoons (85 g) very cold unsalted butter, cut into 1/2-inch (12 mm) cubes
- 3/4 cup (180 mL) cold cultured buttermilk

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 450 °F (230 °C).
2. Whisk the baking powder, flour, and salt until evenly combined.
3. Cut the very cold butter into all of the dry mixture until the butter pieces range from pea-size pieces to flat, thumbnail-size flakes.
4. Fold the cold buttermilk into all of the flour-and-butter mixture until no loose flour remains while the dough still looks shaggy.
5. Cut the dough into 10 biscuits. The image does not specify the dough thickness, cutting shape, cutter size, or pan arrangement.
6. Bake at 450 °F (230 °C) for 12 to 15 minutes. No additional doneness cue is specified.
