# Espresso Brownies

Makes 16 brownies.

## Ingredients

- 115 g unsalted butter
- 60 mL freshly brewed strong espresso
- 200 g granulated sugar
- 2.5 mL vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs
- 1.3 g baking soda
- 80 g Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 80 g all-purpose flour
- 1.5 g fine sea salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 170 °C. Butter and flour an 8 × 8-inch baking pan.
2. Melt the butter.
3. Mix the espresso, melted butter, sugar, and vanilla extract until the mixture is glossy.
4. Whisk in the eggs to make an aerated wet batter.
5. Fold in the baking soda, cocoa powder, flour, and salt to make the espresso brownie batter.
6. Transfer the batter to the prepared pan and bake at 170 °C for 30 to 40 minutes, until a tester emerges with moist crumbs but no raw batter.
