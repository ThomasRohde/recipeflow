# Dual-System Buttermilk Biscuits

Makes 10 biscuits.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon (12 g) baking powder
- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon (6 g) fine salt
- 6 tablespoons (85 g) very cold unsalted butter, cut into 1/2-inch (12 mm) cubes
- 3/4 cup (180 mL) cold cultured buttermilk

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 450 °F (230 °C).
2. Whisk the baking powder, flour, and salt until evenly combined.
3. Cut the cold butter into the dry mixture until the pieces range from pea-size to flat, thumbnail-size flakes.
4. Fold in the buttermilk until no loose flour remains while the dough still looks shaggy.
5. Cut the dough into 10 biscuits. The visible recipe does not specify the shaping, thickness, or cutting method.
6. Bake at 450 °F (230 °C) for 12 to 15 minutes. The visible recipe does not specify a pan or a visual doneness cue.
