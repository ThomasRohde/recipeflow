# Crème brûlée au café – Καραμέλα – コーヒー

Makes 6 ramekins.

## Ingredients

- 60 mL freshly prepared espresso
- 600 mL 35% heavy cream
- 1/2 Madagascar vanilla bean
- 90 g granulated sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- 36 g sugar for caramelizing

## Method

1. Prepare a hot water bath reaching halfway up the sides of six ramekins. Preheat the oven to 150°C.
2. Warm the espresso, cream, and vanilla together for 15 minutes to make a coffee-and-vanilla-infused cream.
3. Gently whisk the infused cream with the granulated sugar and egg yolks until homogeneous, without creating thick foam on the surface.
4. Divide the mixture among the ramekins. Set them in the prepared water bath and bake at 150°C for 35 to 45 minutes, until the edges are set while the centres still tremble like a soft jelly.
5. Let the six creams cool. Use the 36 g caramelizing sugar across their surfaces and caramelize it until each ramekin has a thin, amber, brittle crust with no white sugar visible.
