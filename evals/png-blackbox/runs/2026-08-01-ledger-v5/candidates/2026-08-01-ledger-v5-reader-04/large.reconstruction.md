# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- 3 limes, zest and juice
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided into 30 mL and 45 mL portions
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds
- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt

## Preparations

- Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat for the sauce.
- Preheat a wide, heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately. Use it for the almonds, spices, and chicken.
- Rinse the rice until the water is clear, soak it for 30 minutes, then drain it thoroughly.
- Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls for serving.

## Method

1. Combine the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
2. Blend the cilantro with the lime zest and juice until the sauce is vivid green and pourable, with tiny visible herb flecks remaining. Keep this sauce separate.
3. Put the thoroughly drained rice and 900 mL filtered water in a pot. Steam over low heat for 14 to 18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom.
4. In the warm heavy lidded pot, sauté the garlic, ginger, and onion mixture in 30 mL of the oil over low heat for 10 to 12 minutes, until it becomes a soft golden aromatic base.
5. Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind the spoon.
6. In the preheated wide skillet, toast the almonds over medium heat for 3 to 5 minutes, until evenly golden. Set them aside separately.
7. In the same skillet, toast the coriander and cumin seeds over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, until fragrant. Keep them separate from the other branches.
8. Combine the chicken pieces, salt, toasted coriander and cumin, and yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes.
9. Brown the marinated chicken in batches in the remaining 45 mL oil, using the wide skillet over medium heat, for 12 to 16 minutes, until the pieces are deeply browned.
10. Combine the browned chicken with the tomato and aromatic sauce. Braise over low heat for 25 to 35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.
11. Spread the steamed rice in an even base on the warm platter. Add the chicken so the pieces remain distinct, distribute the cilantro-lime sauce in ribbons, and scatter the toasted almonds on top so they remain crisp.
