# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

8 generous servings

## Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- Zest and juice of 3 limes
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds
- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt

## Method

1. Combine the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
2. Blend the cilantro and the zest and juice of the limes until the sauce is vivid green and pourable while retaining tiny visible herb flecks. Keep this sauce separate.
3. Rinse the rice until the water is clear, soak for 30 minutes, then drain thoroughly.
4. Steam the drained rice with 900 mL filtered water over low heat for 14 to 18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate with no water remaining at the bottom.
5. Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat. Add 30 mL of the neutral cooking oil and the onion, garlic, and ginger mixture. Sauté for 10 to 12 minutes to make a soft golden aromatic base.
6. Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes to the aromatic base. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind the spoon.
7. Preheat a wide heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately. Toast the sliced almonds for 3 to 5 minutes, until evenly golden. Keep them separate.
8. In the same prepared skillet, toast the coriander and cumin seeds for 2 to 3 minutes, until fragrant. Keep them separate.
9. Combine the chicken thigh pieces, salt, all of the toasted cumin and coriander, and the yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes.
10. Brown the marinated chicken in batches in the prepared skillet with the remaining 45 mL neutral cooking oil for 12 to 16 minutes, until deeply browned.
11. Combine the browned chicken and the tomato and aromatic sauce. Braise over low heat for 25 to 35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.
12. Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls. Form an even base of steamed rice on the platter, keeping the chicken distinct. Distribute the herb sauce in ribbons and finish with the crisp toasted almonds on top.
