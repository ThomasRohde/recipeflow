# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- Finely grated zest and strained juice of 2 lemons
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g unsalted butter, softened

## Equipment and setup

- Six clean 180 mL ramekins
- A perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk
- A heavy baking sheet
- A folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer

Chill the clean mixing bowl and whisk for 15 minutes before whipping the meringue.

Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved without delay or obstruction. Beside the oven, make a landing station with the folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, timer, and six clean ramekins.

Preheat the oven to 200 °C with the empty heavy baking sheet on the center rack.

## Method

1. Cook the lemon zest and juice, whole milk, and egg yolks into a thick, warm lemon custard base.
2. In the chilled clean bowl, whip the superfine sugar and egg whites until the meringue is glossy and its peaks stand upright while their tips still bend.
3. Gently fold the softened butter, all of the warm lemon custard base, and all of the meringue together to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture.
4. Fill the six ramekins evenly with all of the mixture, level the tops, and clean the rims.
5. Transfer the ramekins without delay to the preheated heavy baking sheet. Bake at 200 °C for 12 to 14 minutes, until they are six tall lemon soufflés.
6. Serve immediately.
