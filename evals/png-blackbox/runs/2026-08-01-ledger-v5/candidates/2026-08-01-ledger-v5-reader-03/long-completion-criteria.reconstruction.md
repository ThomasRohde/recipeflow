# Slow Caramel with Explicit Completion Criteria

Makes 450 mL sauce.

## Ingredients

- 300 g granulated sugar
- 90 mL filtered water
- 60 g unsalted butter, cut into 6 pieces
- 240 mL warm heavy cream
- 4 g flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Combine the granulated sugar and filtered water. Cook over medium heat without stirring for 4 to 6 minutes, until every visible crystal has disappeared from both the syrup and the inner wall of the pan. The concentrated sugar syrup should be completely clear and look completely transparent against a white spatula.
2. Continue cooking over medium heat for 8 to 12 minutes, swirling occasionally, until the syrup is an even deep amber from edge to center, gives off its first faint thread of smoke, and has no dark, bitter spots.
3. Reduce the heat to low. Carefully whisk the butter pieces, warm heavy cream, and flaky sea salt into the caramel for 3 to 5 minutes. Continue until the bubbling has subsided, every piece of butter is incorporated, and a spoonful cools to a thick ribbon that remains smooth rather than grainy. The finished salted caramel sauce should be smooth with a deep amber finish.
