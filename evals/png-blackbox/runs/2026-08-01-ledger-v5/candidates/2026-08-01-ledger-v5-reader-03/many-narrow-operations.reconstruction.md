# Hand-Folded Dumplings Through Many Narrow Operations

Makes 24 dumplings.

## Ingredients

- 480 g rested dumpling dough
- 360 g chilled cabbage and mushroom filling
- 3 L water, divided into 30 mL for sealing and 2,970 mL for boiling
- 15 mL toasted sesame oil

## Method

1. Divide the rested dumpling dough into 24 equal portions.
2. Round the portions into smooth dough balls.
3. Flatten the balls into thick dough discs.
4. Roll the discs into thin round wrappers.
5. Divide the chilled cabbage and mushroom filling among the wrappers, leaving them open.
6. Use 30 mL of the water to moisten the wrapper rims.
7. Fold the wrappers into half-moons.
8. Pleat and tightly seal the dumplings.
9. Boil the dumplings in the remaining 2,970 mL water for 5 to 7 minutes, until they float and are cooked.
10. Drain the dumplings thoroughly.
11. Toss the drained dumplings with the toasted sesame oil to coat them.
12. Serve the 24 hand-folded mushroom dumplings.
