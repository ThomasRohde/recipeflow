# Caramelized Pear and Hazelnut Celebration Tart with Brown-Butter Custard

Makes one 28 cm tart, serving 10 to 12 people.

## Ingredients

- 180 g unsalted cultured butter, cut into small equal cubes
- 6 large ripe but still firm Conference pears, peeled, cored, and cut lengthwise into evenly sized eighths
- 240 mL heavy cream with at least 35 percent milk fat
- 4 large free-range eggs, at room temperature
- 175 g skinned roasted hazelnuts, with no bitter papery fragments remaining, very finely ground but not processed into nut butter
- 220 g superfine golden caster sugar
- One 28 cm all-butter sweet pastry shell, blind-baked until deeply golden at the edges and completely cooled in its fluted tart pan

## Method

1. Position a rack in the lower third of the oven and preheat the oven without fan assistance to 175 °C. After the thermostat first signals readiness, allow at least 20 minutes for the baking stone and oven walls to reach an even temperature.
2. Arrange the cooled pastry shell on a rimmed tray beside a heatproof landing area. Use a stable work surface protected from hot caramel and splashing custard.
3. Brown the butter slowly for 8 to 12 minutes, until the milk solids are a deep hazelnut brown and smell nutty, without any black specks.
4. Caramelize every surface of the pear wedges for 12 to 15 minutes, until each wedge is amber on both broad sides yet remains firm enough to lift intact.
5. Whisk all of the brown butter with the heavy cream, eggs, ground hazelnuts, and sugar to make a pourable brown-butter and roasted-hazelnut custard. Continue until no dry pockets remain and a ribbon drawn across the surface disappears slowly.
6. Arrange all of the caramelized pear wedges in the cooled pastry shell and pour in all of the custard. Keep the pear fan visible and make sure the custard reaches every gap without overflowing the pastry rim.
7. Carefully transfer the tart to the oven. Bake at 175 °C for 35 to 45 minutes, until the outer custard is set while the center retains a small, coherent wobble when the tray is nudged.
