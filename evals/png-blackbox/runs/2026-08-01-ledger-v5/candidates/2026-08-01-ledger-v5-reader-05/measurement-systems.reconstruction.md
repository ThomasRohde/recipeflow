# Dual-System Buttermilk Biscuits

Makes 10 biscuits.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon (12 g) baking powder
- 1 teaspoon (6 g) fine salt
- 6 tablespoons (85 g) very cold unsalted butter, cut into 1/2-inch (12 mm) cubes
- 3/4 cup (180 mL) cold cultured buttermilk

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 450 °F (230 °C).
2. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt until evenly combined.
3. Cut the cold butter into the dry mixture until the butter pieces range from pea-size pieces to flat, thumbnail-size flakes.
4. Fold the cold buttermilk into the flour mixture until no loose flour remains while the dough still looks shaggy.
5. Cut the dough into 10 biscuits and bake at 450 °F (230 °C) for 12 to 15 minutes.
