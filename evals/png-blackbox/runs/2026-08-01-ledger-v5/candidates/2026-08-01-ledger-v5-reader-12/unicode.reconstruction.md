# Crème brûlée au café · Καραμέλα · コーヒー

Makes 6 ramekins.

## Ingredients

- 60 mL freshly prepared espresso coffee
- 600 mL whole cream with 35% fat
- 1/2 Madagascar vanilla bean
- 90 g granulated sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- 36 g caramelizing sugar

## Method

1. Prepare a hot water bath halfway up six deep ramekins. Preheat the oven to 150 °C.
2. Warm the espresso, cream, and vanilla together for 15 minutes to make a coffee-and-vanilla-infused cream.
3. Gently whisk all of the infused cream with the granulated sugar and egg yolks until the mixture is homogeneous, without thick foam on the surface.
4. Divide the mixture among the six ramekins. Cook in the hot water bath at 150 °C for 35 to 45 minutes, until the edges are set while the centers still wobble like a soft jelly. Let the six creams cool.
5. Distribute the caramelizing sugar over the cooled creams and caramelize until every ramekin has a thin, amber, brittle crust with no visible white sugar.
