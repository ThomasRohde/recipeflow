# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- 3 limes, zest and juice
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds
- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt

## Method

1. Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat. Preheat a wide heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately. Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls.
2. Combine the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
3. Blend the cilantro with the lime zest and juice until the sauce is vivid green and pourable while retaining tiny visible herb flecks. Keep this branch separate.
4. Rinse the rice until the water is clear, soak it for 30 minutes, and drain thoroughly.
5. Steam the drained rice with 900 mL filtered water over low heat for 14 to 18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom.
6. In the warmed heavy lidded pot, sauté the onion, garlic, and ginger mixture with 30 mL of the oil over low heat for 10 to 12 minutes, until it forms a soft golden aromatic base.
7. Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind the spoon.
8. In the preheated skillet, toast the sliced almonds over medium heat for 3 to 5 minutes, until evenly golden. Keep them separate.
9. In the preheated skillet, toast the coriander and cumin seeds over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, until fragrant.
10. Combine the chicken thigh pieces, salt, toasted coriander and cumin, and yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes.
11. Brown the marinated chicken in batches in the preheated skillet with the remaining 45 mL oil over medium heat for 12 to 16 minutes, until deeply browned.
12. Combine the browned chicken with the concentrated tomato and aromatic sauce. Braise over low heat for 25 to 35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.
13. On the warm platter, form an even base of rice. Layer on the chicken while keeping the pieces distinct, distribute the herb sauce in ribbons, and finish with the toasted almonds so they stay crisp. Serve in the warm shallow bowls.
