# Vanilla Custard Base with Two Useful Outputs

Makes one frozen dessert and one pouring sauce.

## Ingredients

- 500 mL heavy cream
- 500 mL whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise
- 180 g granulated sugar
- 8 large egg yolks

## Method

1. Combine the heavy cream, whole milk, and split vanilla bean. Warm and infuse for 20 minutes.
2. Temper and cook the warm vanilla-infused dairy with the granulated sugar and egg yolks at 82 °C, until the custard coats a spoon and a line drawn through it with a finger remains distinct.
3. Divide the smooth cooked vanilla custard into two portions. The proportions are not specified.
4. Chill one portion to make vanilla crème anglaise for pouring.
5. Churn the other portion to make vanilla bean ice cream.
