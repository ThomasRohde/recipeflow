# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Makes 6 glasses.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 300 mL cold heavy cream, divided
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Method

1. Melt the finely chopped dark chocolate until smooth.
2. Divide the cold heavy cream into 250 mL for the mousse base and 50 mL to reserve for the final rosettes.
3. Whip and fold together the melted chocolate, the 250 mL cream, and the superfine sugar until the mixture holds soft peaks and no visible chocolate streaks remain.
4. Portion the airy dark chocolate mousse among six glasses.
5. Garnish with rosettes made from all of the reserved 50 mL cream.
