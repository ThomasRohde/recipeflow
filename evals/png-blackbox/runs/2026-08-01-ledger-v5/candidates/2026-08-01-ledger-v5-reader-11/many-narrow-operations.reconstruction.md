# Hand-Folded Dumplings Through Many Narrow Operations

Makes 24 dumplings.

## Ingredients

- 480 g rested dumpling dough
- 360 g chilled cabbage and mushroom filling
- 3 L water, divided into 30 mL for sealing and 2,970 mL for boiling
- 15 mL toasted sesame oil

The formulas for the dumpling dough and cabbage-and-mushroom filling are not shown; both are ready-made inputs to this recipe.

## Method

1. Divide the rested dumpling dough into 24 equal portions.
2. Round the portions into smooth dough balls.
3. Flatten all of the balls into thick dough discs.
4. Roll all of the discs into thin, round wrappers.
5. Divide the chilled filling among the wrappers, leaving them open.
6. Moisten the wrapper rims with the reserved 30 mL water.
7. Fold the wrappers into half-moons.
8. Pleat and tightly seal the dumplings.
9. Boil the dumplings in the remaining 2,970 mL water for 5 to 7 minutes, until they are cooked and floating.
10. Drain the dumplings thoroughly.
11. Toss all of the drained dumplings with the toasted sesame oil.
12. Serve the 24 hand-folded mushroom dumplings.
