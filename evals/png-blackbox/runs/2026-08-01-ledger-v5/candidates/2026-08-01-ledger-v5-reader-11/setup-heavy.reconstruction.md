# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- 2 lemons, finely grated zest and strained juice
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g unsalted butter, softened

## Standing setup

- Chill a perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes before whipping the meringue.
- Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved without delay or obstruction.
- Beside the oven, arrange a landing station with a folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer.
- Put an empty heavy baking sheet on the center oven rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.
- Arrange six clean 180 mL ramekins on the landing station.

## Method

1. **Cook the lemon base.** Cook the prepared lemon zest and juice, whole milk, and egg yolks into a thick, warm lemon custard base. The image gives no time, heat setting, or more detailed cooking sequence for this step.
2. **Whip.** In the chilled bowl, whip the superfine sugar and egg whites into a glossy, soft-peak meringue. Stop when the glossy peaks stand upright but their tips still bend.
3. **Fold gently.** Gently fold the softened butter, all of the warm lemon custard base, and all of the meringue together to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture. The image does not specify an order for these additions.
4. **Fill and level.** Divide all of the mixture evenly among the six ramekins, level it, and clean the rims.
5. **Transfer and bake.** With the oven approach clear, transfer the ramekins to the preheated heavy baking sheet. Bake at 200 °C for 12 to 14 minutes.
6. Serve the six tall lemon soufflés immediately.
