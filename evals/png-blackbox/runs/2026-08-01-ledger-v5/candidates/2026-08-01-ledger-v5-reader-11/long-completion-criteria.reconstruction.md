# Slow Caramel with Explicit Completion Criteria

Makes 450 mL sauce.

## Ingredients

- 300 g granulated sugar
- 90 mL filtered water
- 60 g unsalted butter, cut into 6 pieces
- 240 mL warm heavy cream
- 4 g flaky sea salt

## Method

1. **Dissolve the sugar without stirring.** Combine the sugar and filtered water and cook over medium heat for 4 to 6 minutes without stirring. Continue until every visible crystal has disappeared from both the syrup and the inner wall of the pan, and the liquid looks completely transparent against a white spatula.
2. **Caramelize, swirling occasionally.** Continue cooking over medium heat for 8 to 12 minutes, swirling occasionally. Stop when the syrup is evenly deep amber from edge to center, gives off its first faint thread of smoke, and has no dark, bitter spots.
3. **Carefully whisk in the dairy and seasoning.** Over low heat, carefully whisk in the butter pieces, warm cream, and flaky sea salt. The image does not specify the order in which to add them. Cook for 3 to 5 minutes, until the bubbling has subsided, every piece of butter is incorporated, and a spoonful cools to a thick ribbon that remains smooth rather than grainy.

The finished sauce should be smooth, salted, and deep amber.
