# Vanilla Custard Base with Two Useful Outputs

Makes one frozen dessert and one pouring sauce; the amount of each is not specified.

## Ingredients

- 500 mL heavy cream
- 500 mL whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise
- 180 g granulated sugar
- 8 large egg yolks

## Recipe

1. Combine the heavy cream, whole milk, and split vanilla bean. Warm and infuse for 20 minutes to make warm vanilla-infused dairy.
2. Temper and cook the granulated sugar and egg yolks with all of the warm vanilla-infused dairy. Heat to 82 °C, until the custard coats a spoon and a line drawn through it with a finger remains distinct.
3. Divide the smooth cooked vanilla custard into two portions; the proportions are not specified.
4. Chill one portion to make vanilla crème anglaise for pouring.
5. Churn the other portion to make vanilla bean ice cream.
