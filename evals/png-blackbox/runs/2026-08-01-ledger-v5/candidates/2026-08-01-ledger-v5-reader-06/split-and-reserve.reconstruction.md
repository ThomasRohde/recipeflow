# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Makes 6 glasses.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 300 mL cold heavy cream, divided into 250 mL and 50 mL
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Recipe

1. Melt the finely chopped dark chocolate until smooth.
2. Divide the cold heavy cream: use 250 mL for the mousse base and hold back 50 mL for the final rosettes.
3. Whip and fold the smooth melted chocolate, 250 mL cream, and superfine sugar together until the mixture holds soft peaks and has no visible chocolate streaks.
4. Portion the airy dark chocolate mousse among six glasses.
5. Garnish each glass with a rosette made from the reserved cream.
