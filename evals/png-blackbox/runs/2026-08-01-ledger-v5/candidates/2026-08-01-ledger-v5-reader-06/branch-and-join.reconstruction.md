# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Makes 4 bowls.

## Ingredients

- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- 4 garlic cloves
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- Salted water, for boiling the pasta (amount not specified)

## Recipe

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
3. Add the dried rigatoni to the boiling salted water and boil for 10–12 minutes, until al dente.
4. Tear the basil leaves and toss them with the finely grated Parmesan to make a basil and Parmesan finishing mixture. Keep this separate.
5. Roast the garlic cloves and cherry tomatoes in the preheated oven for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed. Keep this separate from the pasta and finishing mixture.
6. Crush all of the roasted garlic and tomatoes into a rustic roasted tomato sauce.
7. Toss all of the al dente rigatoni, rustic roasted tomato sauce, and basil and Parmesan finishing mixture together until every tube of pasta is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed.
8. Divide among four bowls.
