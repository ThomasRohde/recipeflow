# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Makes 4 bowls.

## Ingredients

- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- 4 garlic cloves
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- A large pot of salted water; the amounts of water and salt are not specified

## Method

1. Bring the large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
3. Boil the rigatoni in the salted water for 10 to 12 minutes, until al dente.
4. Meanwhile, tear the basil leaves and toss them with the finely grated Parmesan to make a finishing mixture.
5. Roast the garlic cloves and cherry tomatoes in the preheated oven for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed.
6. Crush all of the roasted garlic and tomatoes into a rustic roasted tomato sauce.
7. Toss all of the al dente rigatoni, all of the sauce, and all of the basil-Parmesan mixture together until every pasta tube is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed.
8. Divide among four bowls.

The visible recipe does not specify roasting equipment, whether anything is added to the tomatoes and garlic before roasting, or whether the pasta should be drained before tossing.
