# Vanilla Custard Base with Two Useful Outputs

Makes one frozen dessert and one pouring sauce; the quantities of each are not specified.

## Ingredients

- 500 mL heavy cream
- 500 mL whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise
- 180 g granulated sugar
- 8 large egg yolks

## Method

1. Combine the heavy cream, whole milk, and split vanilla bean. Warm and infuse for 20 minutes.
2. Temper and cook all of the warm vanilla-infused dairy with the sugar and egg yolks, heating the custard to 82 °C. Continue until it coats a spoon and a line drawn through the coating with a finger remains distinct.
3. Divide the smooth cooked vanilla custard into two portions. The proportion for each portion is not specified.
4. Chill one portion to make crème anglaise for pouring.
5. Churn the other portion to make vanilla bean ice cream.
