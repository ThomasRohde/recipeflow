# Hand-Folded Dumplings Through Many Narrow Operations

Makes 24 dumplings.

## Ingredients

- 480 g rested dumpling dough
- 360 g chilled cabbage and mushroom filling
- 3 L water for sealing and boiling, divided into 30 mL and 2,970 mL
- 15 mL toasted sesame oil

## Method

1. Divide the rested dumpling dough into 24 equal portions.
2. Round all of the portions into smooth dough balls.
3. Flatten all of the balls into thick dough discs.
4. Roll all of the discs into thin, round wrappers.
5. Divide the chilled cabbage and mushroom filling among all 24 wrappers, leaving them open.
6. Use 30 mL of the water to moisten the wrappers' rims.
7. Fold the wrappers into half-moons.
8. Pleat and tightly seal the dumplings.
9. Boil the dumplings in the remaining 2,970 mL water for 5–7 minutes, until cooked and floating.
10. Drain the dumplings thoroughly.
11. Toss all of the drained dumplings with the toasted sesame oil to coat them.
12. Serve all 24 dumplings.
