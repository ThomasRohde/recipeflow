# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- Finely grated zest and strained juice of 2 lemons
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g softened unsalted butter
- Additional butter and sugar for preparing the ramekins; quantities are not specified

## Setup

- Use six 180 mL ramekins.
- Chill a perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes before whipping the meringue.
- Brush the ramekins upward with butter, then coat every interior with sugar.
- Arrange a folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven.
- Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved without delay or obstruction.
- Put an empty heavy baking sheet on the center rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.

## Method

1. **Cook the lemon base.** Use the lemon zest and juice, whole milk, and egg yolks to make a thick, warm lemon custard base. The image does not specify the heat level, cooking time, ingredient order, or a more precise completion test for this step.
2. **Whip the meringue.** In the chilled bowl, whip the superfine sugar and egg whites until the mixture is glossy and the peaks stand upright while their tips still bend.
3. **Fold gently.** Gently fold together all of the warm lemon custard base, all of the glossy soft-peak meringue, and the 30 g softened butter to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture. The image does not specify an order for combining these three components.
4. **Fill and level.** Divide all of the mixture evenly among the six prepared ramekins. Level the mixture and clean the rims.
5. **Transfer and bake.** Without delay, move the filled ramekins to the preheated heavy baking sheet. Bake at 200 °C for 12–14 minutes, producing six tall lemon soufflés.
6. Serve immediately.
