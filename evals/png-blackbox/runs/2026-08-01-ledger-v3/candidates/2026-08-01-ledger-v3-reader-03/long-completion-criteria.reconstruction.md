# Slow Caramel with Explicit Completion Criteria

Makes 450 mL sauce.

## Ingredients

- 300 g granulated sugar
- 90 mL filtered water
- 60 g unsalted butter, cut into 6 pieces
- 240 mL warm heavy cream
- 4 g flaky sea salt

## Method

1. **Dissolve the sugar without stirring.** Combine the granulated sugar and filtered water and cook over medium heat for 4–6 minutes, without stirring. Continue until every visible crystal has disappeared from both the syrup and the inner wall of the pan, and the liquid looks completely transparent when viewed against a white spatula. The result should be a completely clear, concentrated sugar syrup.
2. **Caramelize, swirling occasionally.** Continue cooking the syrup over medium heat for 8–12 minutes, swirling occasionally. Stop when the syrup is an even deep amber from edge to center, gives off its first faint thread of smoke, and has not developed any dark, bitter spots.
3. **Carefully whisk in the dairy and seasoning.** Add the butter pieces, warm heavy cream, and flaky sea salt to the deep-amber caramel. Whisk carefully over low heat for 3–5 minutes. Continue until the bubbling has subsided, every piece of butter is incorporated, and a spoonful cools to a thick ribbon that remains smooth rather than grainy.

The finished sauce should be smooth and salted, with a deep amber color.
