# Cinnamon Toast

Makes 2 slices.

## Ingredients

- 2 slices sourdough bread
- 2 tablespoons cinnamon butter

## Preparation

Toast the sourdough bread with the cinnamon butter for 3–5 minutes to make 2 slices of cinnamon toast.
