# Crème brûlée au café

Makes 6 ramekins.

## Ingredients

- 60 mL freshly prepared espresso coffee
- 600 mL whole cream with 35% milk fat
- 1/2 Madagascar vanilla bean
- 90 g granulated sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- 36 g sugar for caramelizing

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 150 °C.
2. Prepare a hot water bath in a deep dish, with enough hot water to come halfway up the ramekins.
3. Warm the espresso, cream, and vanilla bean together for 15 minutes to make a coffee-and-vanilla-infused cream.
4. Gently whisk all of the infused cream with the granulated sugar and egg yolks until the mixture is homogeneous, without a thick layer of foam on the surface.
5. Divide the mixture among 6 ramekins. Set them in the prepared hot water bath and bake at 150 °C for 35–45 minutes, until the edges are set while the centers still jiggle like a soft jelly.
6. Cool the custards. The image does not specify a cooling method or duration.
7. Distribute the 36 g caramelizing sugar over the 6 cooled custards and caramelize it until each ramekin has a thin, golden, crisp crust with no visible white sugar.
