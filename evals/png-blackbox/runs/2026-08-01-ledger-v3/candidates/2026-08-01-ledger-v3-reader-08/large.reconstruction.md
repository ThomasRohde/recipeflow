# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- 3 limes, zest and juice
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided: 30 mL for the aromatic base and 45 mL for browning the chicken
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds
- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt

## Advance preparation

1. Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat for the sauce.
2. Preheat a wide, heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately.
3. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, soak it for 30 minutes, and drain it thoroughly.
4. Warm a broad serving platter and 8 shallow bowls.

## Method

1. **Combine the aromatics.** Mix the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
2. **Blend the herb sauce.** Blend the cilantro with the lime zest and juice until the sauce is vivid green and pourable while retaining tiny visible herb flecks. Set it aside as a separate component.
3. **Steam the rice.** Combine the thoroughly drained soaked basmati rice with 900 mL filtered water. Cook over low heat for 14–18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom. Keep the rice for serving.
4. **Sauté the aromatics.** In the warmed heavy lidded pot, cook the onion, garlic, and ginger mixture with 30 mL of the oil over low heat for 10–12 minutes, until it becomes a soft, golden aromatic base.
5. **Simmer the sauce.** Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes to the aromatic base. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind a spoon.
6. **Toast the almonds.** In the preheated wide, heavy skillet over medium heat, toast the sliced almonds for 3–5 minutes, until evenly golden. Set them aside as a separate component.
7. **Toast the spices.** In the preheated skillet over medium heat, toast the coriander and cumin seeds for 2–3 minutes, until fragrant. Set them aside.
8. **Marinate the chicken.** Combine the chicken thigh pieces with the salt, all of the toasted cumin and coriander, and the yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes.
9. **Brown the chicken.** Using the preheated skillet over medium heat and the remaining 45 mL oil, brown the marinated chicken in batches for 12–16 minutes, until the pieces are deeply browned.
10. **Braise the chicken.** Combine all of the browned chicken with all of the concentrated tomato and aromatic sauce. Braise over low heat for 25–35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.
11. **Layer and finish.** On the warm platter, form an even base of rice. Arrange the chicken so the pieces remain distinct, distribute the herb sauce in ribbons, and top with the toasted almonds so they stay crisp. Serve in the warmed shallow bowls.
