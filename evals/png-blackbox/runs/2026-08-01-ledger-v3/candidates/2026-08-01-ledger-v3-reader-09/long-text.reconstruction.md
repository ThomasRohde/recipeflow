# Caramelized Pear and Hazelnut Celebration Tart with Brown-Butter Custard

Makes one 28 cm tart, serving 10–12 people.

## Ingredients

- 180 g unsalted cultured butter, cut into small equal cubes
- 6 large, ripe but still firm Conference pears, peeled, cored, and cut lengthwise into evenly sized eighths
- 240 mL heavy cream with at least 35% milk fat
- 4 large free-range eggs, at room temperature
- 175 g skinned roasted hazelnuts, very finely ground but not processed into nut butter, with no bitter papery fragments remaining
- 220 g superfine golden caster sugar
- One 28 cm all-butter sweet pastry shell, blind-baked until deeply golden at the edges and completely cooled in its fluted tart pan

## Method

1. Position an oven rack in the lower third and preheat the oven, without fan assistance, to 175 °C. After the thermostat first signals that the oven is ready, allow at least 20 more minutes for the referenced baking stone and oven walls to reach an even temperature.
2. Brown the cubed butter slowly for 8–12 minutes, until the milk solids are a deep hazelnut brown and smell nutty, with no black specks. Retain all of the fragrant brown butter and toasted milk solids.
3. In a separate preparation, caramelize every surface of the pear wedges for 12–15 minutes, until each wedge is amber on two broad sides but remains firm enough to lift intact.
4. Whisk all of the brown butter and toasted milk solids with the heavy cream, eggs, finely ground roasted hazelnuts, and superfine golden caster sugar. Continue until no dry pockets remain and a ribbon drawn across the surface disappears slowly; the custard should be pourable.
5. Arrange the cooled pastry shell, still in its fluted tart pan, on a rimmed tray beside a heatproof landing area. Use a stable work surface protected from hot caramel and splashing custard.
6. Arrange all of the caramelized pear wedges in the shell and pour in all of the custard. Keep the pear fan visible and let the custard reach every gap without overflowing the pastry rim.
7. Bake on the lower-third rack at 175 °C for 35–45 minutes, until the outer custard is set while the center retains a small, coherent wobble when the tray is nudged.
