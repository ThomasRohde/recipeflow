# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Makes 6 glasses.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 300 mL cold heavy cream, divided into 250 mL and 50 mL
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Method

1. Melt the finely chopped dark chocolate until smooth. The melting method and temperature are not specified.
2. Set aside 250 mL of the cream for the mousse base. Reserve the remaining 50 mL for the final rosettes.
3. Whip and fold together the smooth melted chocolate, the 250 mL cream, and the superfine sugar until the mixture holds soft peaks and has no visible chocolate streaks. The exact order for whipping and folding the ingredients is not specified.
4. Portion the airy dark chocolate mousse among six glasses.
5. Garnish the glasses with rosettes made from the reserved 50 mL cream. The recipe does not say whether to whip the reserved cream first or how to form the rosettes.

No chilling or resting time is specified.
