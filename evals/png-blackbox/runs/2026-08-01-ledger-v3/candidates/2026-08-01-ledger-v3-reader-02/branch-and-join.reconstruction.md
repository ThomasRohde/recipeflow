# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Makes 4 bowls.

## Ingredients

- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- 4 garlic cloves
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- A large pot of salted water (the amount of water and salt are not specified)

## Method

1. Bring the large pot of salted water to a boil. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
2. Boil the rigatoni for 10–12 minutes, until al dente. The recipe does not specify whether or how thoroughly to drain it.
3. Tear the basil leaves and toss them with the finely grated Parmesan to make a basil-and-Parmesan finishing mixture.
4. Roast the garlic cloves and cherry tomatoes in the preheated oven for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed. No oil, seasoning, pan, or preparation of the garlic and tomatoes is specified.
5. Crush all of the roasted garlic and tomatoes into a rustic roasted tomato sauce. The recipe does not specify how finely to crush them.
6. Toss together all of the al dente rigatoni, all of the rustic roasted tomato sauce, and all of the basil-and-Parmesan mixture. Continue until every tube is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed.
7. Divide among four bowls.
