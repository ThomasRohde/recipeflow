# Vanilla Custard Base with Two Useful Outputs

Makes one frozen dessert and one pouring sauce; individual quantities are not specified.

## Ingredients

- 500 mL heavy cream
- 500 mL whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise
- 180 g granulated sugar
- 8 large egg yolks

## Method

1. Combine the heavy cream, whole milk, and split vanilla bean. Warm and infuse for 20 minutes. The exact temperature is not specified, and the recipe does not say whether to remove the vanilla bean afterward.
2. Use all of the warm vanilla-infused dairy with the sugar and egg yolks to temper and cook a smooth vanilla custard. Heat the custard to 82 °C, until it coats a spoon and a line drawn through it with a finger remains distinct. The exact tempering sequence is not specified.
3. Divide the cooked custard into two portions. No split ratio is given.
4. Chill one portion to make vanilla crème anglaise for pouring.
5. Churn the other portion to make vanilla bean ice cream. Chilling, churning, and freezing times and equipment are not specified.
