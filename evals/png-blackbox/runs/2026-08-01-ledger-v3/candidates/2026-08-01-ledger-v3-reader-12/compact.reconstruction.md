# Cinnamon Toast

Makes 2 slices.

## Ingredients

- 2 slices sourdough bread
- 2 tablespoons cinnamon butter

## Method

1. Toast the two slices of sourdough bread with the cinnamon butter for 3.5 minutes, producing two slices of cinnamon toast.
