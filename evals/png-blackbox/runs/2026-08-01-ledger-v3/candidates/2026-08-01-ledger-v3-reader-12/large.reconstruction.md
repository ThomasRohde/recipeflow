# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

### Aromatic base and tomato sauce

- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 30 mL neutral cooking oil
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes

### Herb sauce

- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- Zest and juice of 3 limes

### Rice

- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water

### Chicken

- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt
- 45 mL neutral cooking oil

### Finish

- 120 g blanched sliced almonds

The total neutral cooking oil shown is 75 mL, divided into 30 mL for the aromatic base and 45 mL for browning the chicken.

## Preparation

1. Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat for the sauce.
2. Preheat a wide, heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately.
3. Rinse the basmati rice until the water runs clear. Soak it for 30 minutes, then drain it thoroughly.
4. Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls for serving. The image gives no warming time or temperature.

## Method

1. **Combine the aromatics.** Mix the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
2. **Blend the herb sauce.** Blend the cilantro with the lime zest and juice until the sauce is vivid green and pourable but still has tiny visible herb flecks. Set it aside.
3. **Steam the rice.** Put all the thoroughly drained rice and 900 mL filtered water over low heat. Steam for 14–18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom. The image does not give any further pot or lid instructions.
4. **Sauté the aromatics.** Put the aromatic mixture and 30 mL neutral oil into the warmed heavy pot. Cook over low heat for 10–12 minutes, until it becomes a soft, golden aromatic base.
5. **Simmer the sauce.** Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes to the aromatic base. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind a spoon.
6. **Toast the almonds.** In the preheated wide skillet over medium heat, toast the sliced almonds for 3–5 minutes, until evenly golden. Set them aside.
7. **Toast the spices.** In the same preheated skillet over medium heat, toast the whole coriander and cumin seeds for 2–3 minutes, until fragrant.
8. **Marinate the chicken.** Combine the chicken pieces, salt, all the toasted coriander and cumin, and yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes.
9. **Brown the chicken.** Using the wide skillet over medium heat, brown the marinated chicken in batches with 45 mL neutral oil for 12–16 minutes, until the pieces are deeply browned.
10. **Braise the chicken.** Combine all the browned chicken with all the concentrated tomato and aromatic sauce. Braise over low heat for 25–35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.
11. **Layer and finish.** On the warm broad platter, make an even base of all the steamed rice. Arrange all the chicken braise so the pieces remain distinct. Distribute all the cilantro-lime sauce in ribbons, then top with all the toasted almonds so they remain crisp.

Serve from the warm platter in the eight warm shallow bowls. The image specifies no additional seasoning, garnish, or serving accompaniment.
