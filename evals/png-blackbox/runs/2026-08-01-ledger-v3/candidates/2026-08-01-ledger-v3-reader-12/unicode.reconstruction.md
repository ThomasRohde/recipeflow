# Crème brûlée au café

Makes 6 ramekins.

## Ingredients

- 60 mL freshly prepared espresso coffee
- 600 mL 35% heavy cream
- 1/2 Madagascar vanilla bean
- 90 g granulated sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- 36 g sugar for caramelizing

## Method

1. Prepare a hot water bath: place the six ramekins in a deep dish and add hot water to reach halfway up their sides.
2. Preheat the oven to 150 °C.
3. Warm the espresso, cream, and vanilla bean together for 15 minutes to make a coffee-and-vanilla-infused cream. The image does not specify an exact temperature or heating vessel for this infusion.
4. Gently whisk all the infused cream with the granulated sugar and egg yolks until the mixture is homogeneous, without a thick layer of foam on the surface.
5. Divide the custard mixture among the six ramekins. Bake them in the prepared water bath at 150 °C for 35–45 minutes, until the edges are set while the centers still tremble like a soft jelly.
