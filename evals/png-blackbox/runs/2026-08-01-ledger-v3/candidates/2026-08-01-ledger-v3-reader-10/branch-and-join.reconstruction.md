# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Makes 4 bowls.

## Ingredients

- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- 4 garlic cloves
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- Salted water, quantity and salt amount not specified

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
3. Boil the rigatoni for 10–12 minutes, until al dente.
4. In a separate branch of the preparation, tear the basil leaves and toss them with the finely grated Parmesan to make a finishing mixture.
5. In another separate branch, roast the garlic cloves and cherry tomatoes in the preheated oven for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed. The image does not specify a pan, added fat, seasoning, or other roasting details.
6. Crush all of the collapsed garlic-roasted tomatoes to make a rustic roasted tomato sauce. The image does not specify the crushing tool or desired texture beyond “rustic.”
7. Toss together all of the al dente rigatoni, all of the roasted tomato sauce, and all of the basil-Parmesan mixture. Continue until every tube is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed.
8. Divide among four bowls.
