# Vanilla Custard Base with Two Useful Outputs

Makes one frozen dessert and one pouring sauce; exact quantities are not specified.

## Ingredients

- 500 mL heavy cream
- 500 mL whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise
- 180 g granulated sugar
- 8 large egg yolks
