# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Makes 6 glasses.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 300 mL cold heavy cream, divided into 250 mL and 50 mL
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Method

1. Melt the finely chopped dark chocolate until smooth. The image does not specify the melting method or temperature.
2. Divide the cold heavy cream: set aside 250 mL for the mousse base and reserve the remaining 50 mL for the final rosettes.
3. Whip and fold together the smooth melted chocolate, the 250 mL cream for the mousse base, and the superfine sugar. Continue until the mixture holds soft peaks and no visible chocolate streaks remain. The image does not give a more detailed mixing order.
4. Portion the airy dark chocolate mousse into six glasses.
