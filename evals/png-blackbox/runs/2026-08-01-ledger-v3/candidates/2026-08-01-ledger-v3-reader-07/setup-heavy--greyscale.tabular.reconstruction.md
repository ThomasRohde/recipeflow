# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- 2 lemons, finely grated zest and strained juice
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g unsalted butter, softened, plus an unspecified amount for brushing the ramekins
- Sugar for coating the ramekins, amount unspecified

## Equipment and advance setup

- 6 ramekins, 180 mL each
- A perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk
- A heavy baking sheet
- A folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer

1. Chill the clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes before whipping the egg whites.
2. Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved without delay or obstruction.
3. Set the folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven as a landing station.
4. Put the empty heavy baking sheet on the center rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.
5. Brush the six ramekins upward with butter, then coat every interior with sugar.

1. **Cook the lemon base.** Use the lemon zest and juice, whole milk, and egg yolks to make a thick, warm lemon custard base. The image does not state the mixing order, cooking temperature, cooking time, or exact completion test for this step.
2. **Whip the meringue.** Whip the superfine sugar and egg whites in the chilled bowl with the chilled whisk until the meringue is glossy and its peaks stand upright while their tips still bend.
3. **Fold gently.** Gently fold together all of the warm lemon custard base, all of the glossy soft-peak meringue, and the 30 g softened butter to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture. The image does not specify an order for adding these components.
4. **Fill and level.** Divide the mixture evenly among the six prepared ramekins. Level the mixture and clean the rims.
5. **Transfer and bake.** With the oven approach clear, transfer the ramekins to the 200 °C oven and bake for 12–14 minutes. The preheated heavy baking sheet is required for this step, but the image does not explicitly state whether the ramekins sit directly on it.
6. Serve the six tall lemon soufflés immediately.
