# Hand-Folded Dumplings Through Many Narrow Operations

Makes 24 dumplings.

## Ingredients

- 480 g rested dumpling dough
- 360 g chilled cabbage and mushroom filling
- 3 L water, divided: 30 mL for sealing and 2,970 mL for boiling
- 15 mL toasted sesame oil

## Method

1. Divide the rested dumpling dough into 24 equal portions.
2. Round all 24 portions into smooth dough balls.
3. Flatten all the balls into thick dough discs.
4. Roll all the discs into thin, round wrappers.
5. Distribute the chilled cabbage and mushroom filling among the wrappers, leaving them open after filling. The image does not give a measured amount of filling for each wrapper.
6. Use the 30 mL portion of water to moisten the wrapper rims.
7. Fold the moistened wrappers into half-moons.
8. Pleat the half-moons so the dumplings are tightly sealed.
9. Boil the dumplings in the remaining 2,970 mL water for 5–7 minutes, until they float.
10. Drain the cooked dumplings thoroughly.
11. Toss all the drained dumplings with the toasted sesame oil until coated.
12. Serve the 24 hand-folded mushroom dumplings.
