# Slow Caramel with Explicit Completion Criteria

Makes 450 mL sauce.

## Ingredients

- 300 g granulated sugar
- 90 mL filtered water
- 60 g unsalted butter, cut into 6 pieces
- 240 mL warm heavy cream
- 4 g flaky sea salt

## Method

1. **Dissolve the sugar without stirring.** Combine the granulated sugar and filtered water and cook over medium heat for 4–6 minutes without stirring. Continue until every visible crystal has disappeared from both the syrup and the inner wall of the pan, and the liquid looks completely transparent when viewed against a white spatula.
2. **Caramelize.** Keep cooking over medium heat for 8–12 minutes, swirling occasionally. Stop when the caramel is evenly deep amber from edge to center, gives off its first faint thread of smoke, and has no dark, bitter spots.
3. **Whisk in the dairy and seasoning carefully.** Add the butter, warm heavy cream, and flaky sea salt, and whisk carefully over low heat for 3–5 minutes. Continue until the bubbling has subsided, every piece of butter is incorporated, and a spoonful cools to a thick ribbon that remains smooth rather than grainy.

The finished sauce should be smooth and salted, with a deep amber color.
