# Hand-Folded Dumplings Through Many Narrow Operations

Makes 24 dumplings.

## Ingredients

- 480 g rested dumpling dough
- 360 g chilled cabbage and mushroom filling
- 3 L water for sealing and boiling, divided into 30 mL for moistening the rims and 2,970 mL for boiling
- 15 mL toasted sesame oil

## Method

1. Divide the rested dumpling dough into 24 equal portions.
2. Round all 24 portions into smooth dough balls.
3. Flatten all of the balls into thick dough discs.
4. Roll all of the discs into thin, round wrappers.
5. Divide the chilled cabbage and mushroom filling among the wrappers, leaving them open.
6. Use 30 mL of the water to moisten the rims of all the filled wrappers.
7. Fold the wrappers into half-moons.
8. Pleat the half-moons and seal them tightly.
9. Boil the tightly sealed dumplings in the remaining 2,970 mL water for 5–7 minutes, until they are cooked and floating. The image does not specify a burner setting.
10. Drain the cooked dumplings thoroughly.
11. Toss all of the drained dumplings with the toasted sesame oil.
12. Serve the 24 hand-folded mushroom dumplings.
