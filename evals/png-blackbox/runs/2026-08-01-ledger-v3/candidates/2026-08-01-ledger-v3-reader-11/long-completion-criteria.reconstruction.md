# Slow Caramel with Explicit Completion Criteria

Makes 450 mL sauce.

## Ingredients

- 300 g granulated sugar
- 90 mL filtered water
- 60 g unsalted butter, cut into 6 pieces
- 240 mL warm heavy cream
- 4 g flaky sea salt

## Method

1. **Dissolve the sugar without stirring.** Combine the granulated sugar and filtered water and heat over medium heat for 4–6 minutes without stirring. Continue until every visible crystal has disappeared from both the syrup and the inner wall of the pan, and the liquid is completely transparent when viewed against a white spatula.
2. **Caramelize while swirling occasionally.** Keep the clear, concentrated sugar syrup over medium heat for 8–12 minutes, swirling occasionally. Continue until the syrup is an even deep amber from edge to center, gives off its first faint thread of smoke, and has not developed any dark, bitter spots.
3. **Carefully whisk in the dairy and seasoning.** Over low heat, carefully whisk the butter, warm heavy cream, and flaky sea salt into the deep-amber caramel. Cook for 3–5 minutes, until the bubbling has subsided, every piece of butter is incorporated, and a spoonful cools to a thick ribbon that remains smooth rather than grainy.
4. The result should be a smooth salted caramel sauce with a deep amber finish.
