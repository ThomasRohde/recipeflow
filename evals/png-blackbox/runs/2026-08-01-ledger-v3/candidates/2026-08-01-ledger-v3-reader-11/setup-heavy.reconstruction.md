# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- 2 lemons, using the finely grated zest and strained juice
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g softened unsalted butter
- Additional butter and sugar for coating the ramekins; quantities are not specified

## Equipment and advance setup

- Six 180 mL ramekins
- A perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk
- A heavy baking sheet
- A folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer

1. Chill the perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes before whipping the meringue.
2. Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved to it without delay or obstruction.
3. Arrange the folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven before filling the ramekins.
4. Put the empty heavy baking sheet on the center rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.
5. Brush the six ramekins upward with butter, then coat every interior with sugar.

## Method

1. **Cook the lemon base.** Combine the finely grated zest and strained juice of the 2 lemons with the whole milk and egg yolks. Cook them to make a thick, warm lemon custard base. The image does not specify the cooking temperature, time, or more detailed technique.
2. **Whip the meringue.** In the chilled clean bowl, use the chilled balloon whisk to whip the superfine sugar and egg whites into a glossy soft-peak meringue. Stop when glossy peaks stand upright but their tips still bend.
3. **Fold gently.** Gently fold together all of the warm lemon custard base, all of the glossy soft-peak meringue, and the softened butter to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture. The image does not specify an order for adding these three components.
4. **Fill and level.** Using the prepared landing station and coated ramekins, divide all of the mixture evenly among the six ramekins and level it. Clean the rims so you have six evenly filled, rim-cleaned ramekins.
5. **Transfer and bake.** With the oven approach clear, transfer the ramekins to the preheated oven and bake on the hot heavy baking sheet at 200 °C for 12–14 minutes.
6. Serve the six tall lemon soufflés immediately.
