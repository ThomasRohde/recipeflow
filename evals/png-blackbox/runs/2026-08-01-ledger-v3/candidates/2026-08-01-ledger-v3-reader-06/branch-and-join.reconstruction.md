# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Makes 4 bowls.

## Ingredients

- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- 4 garlic cloves
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- Salted water for boiling; the amount of water and salt are not specified

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
3. Boil the rigatoni for 10–12 minutes, until al dente.
4. In a separate branch of the preparation, tear the basil leaves and toss them with the finely grated Parmesan to make a finishing mixture.
5. In another separate branch, roast the garlic cloves and cherry tomatoes in the preheated oven for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed. The image does not specify a pan, added fat, seasoning, or whether the garlic should be peeled.
6. Crush all of the roasted garlic and tomatoes into a rustic roasted tomato sauce.
7. Toss together all of the al dente rigatoni, all of the rustic roasted tomato sauce, and all of the basil-Parmesan finishing mixture. Continue until every tube of pasta is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed.
8. Divide into four bowls.
