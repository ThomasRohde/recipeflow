# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Makes 6 glasses.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 300 mL cold heavy cream, divided into 250 mL and 50 mL
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Method

1. Melt the finely chopped dark chocolate until smooth.
2. Divide the cold heavy cream: set aside 250 mL for the mousse base and hold the remaining 50 mL for the final rosettes.
3. Whip and fold the melted chocolate, the 250 mL portion of cream, and the superfine sugar into an airy dark chocolate mousse.
