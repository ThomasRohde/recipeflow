# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- 3 limes, zest and juice
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided: 30 mL for the aromatic base and 45 mL for browning the chicken
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds
- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt

## Advance preparation

1. Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat for the sauce branch.
2. Preheat a wide, heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately.
3. Rinse the basmati rice until the water runs clear, soak it for 30 minutes, and then drain it thoroughly.
4. Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls.

## Method

1. **Combine the aromatics.** Mix the finely chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
2. **Blend the herb sauce.** Blend the cilantro with the zest and juice of all 3 limes until the sauce is vivid green and pourable while retaining tiny visible herb flecks. Keep this sauce separate.
3. **Steam the rice.** Put the thoroughly drained soaked rice and 900 mL filtered water together and cook over low heat for 14–18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom. Keep the cooked rice for assembly.
4. **Sauté the aromatics.** In the warmed heavy lidded pot, cook all of the garlic-ginger-onion mixture with 30 mL of the neutral oil for 10–12 minutes, making a soft, golden aromatic base.
5. **Simmer the sauce.** Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes to the aromatic base. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind the spoon. Keep this concentrated tomato and aromatic sauce.
6. **Toast the almonds.** In the preheated wide skillet, toast the sliced almonds over medium heat for 3–5 minutes, until evenly golden. Keep them separate.
7. **Toast the spices.** In the same preheated skillet, toast the whole coriander and cumin seeds over medium heat for 2–3 minutes, until fragrant.
8. **Marinate the chicken.** Combine the chicken thigh pieces, salt, all of the toasted cumin and coriander, and the yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes.
9. **Brown the chicken.** Brown all of the marinated chicken in batches in the wide skillet with the remaining 45 mL neutral oil. Allow 12–16 minutes in total, producing deeply browned chicken pieces.
10. **Braise the chicken.** Combine the browned chicken with all of the concentrated tomato and aromatic sauce. Cook over low heat for 25–35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.
11. **Layer and finish.** On the warm broad platter, form an even base with all of the steamed rice. Add all of the chicken braise while keeping the chicken pieces distinct. Distribute all of the bright cilantro-lime sauce in ribbons, then top with all of the toasted almonds so they remain crisp.

Serve with the eight warm shallow bowls.
