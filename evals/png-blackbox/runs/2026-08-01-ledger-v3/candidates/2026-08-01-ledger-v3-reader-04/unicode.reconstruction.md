# Crème brûlée au café

Makes 6 ramequins.

## Ingredients

- 60 mL freshly brewed espresso
- 600 mL 35% fat heavy cream
- 1/2 Madagascar vanilla bean
- 90 g granulated sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- 36 g sugar for caramelizing

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 150 °C.
2. Prepare a hot water bath in a deep dish, using enough hot water to reach halfway up the sides of six ramequins.
3. Warm the espresso, cream, and vanilla together for 15 minutes to make a coffee-and-vanilla-infused cream. The image specifies warm heat but no more exact temperature.
4. Gently whisk all of the infused cream with the 90 g sugar and the 6 egg yolks. Continue until the mixture is homogeneous, without a thick layer of foam on the surface.
5. Divide the mixture among the six ramequins and place them in the prepared water bath. This division is implied by the six-ramequin yield and the water-bath preparation; the image does not state it as a separate instruction.
6. Bake gently at 150 °C for 35–45 minutes, until the edges are set but the centers still wobble like a soft jelly.
7. Cool the six custards. No cooling time or method is given.
8. Distribute the 36 g caramelizing sugar over the cooled custards and caramelize it until every ramequin has a thin, amber, brittle crust with no visible white sugar. The image does not specify whether to use a torch, broiler, or another method.
