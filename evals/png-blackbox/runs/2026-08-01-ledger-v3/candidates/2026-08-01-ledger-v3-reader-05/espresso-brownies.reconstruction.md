# Espresso Brownies

Makes 16 brownies.

## Ingredients

- 115 g unsalted butter
- 60 mL freshly brewed strong espresso
- 200 g granulated sugar
- 2.5 mL vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs
- 1.3 g baking soda
- 80 g Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 80 g all-purpose flour
- 1.5 g fine sea salt
- Additional butter and flour for the pan; quantities are not specified

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 170 °C. Butter and flour an 8 × 8 inch baking pan.
2. Melt the 115 g butter.
3. Mix the melted butter with the espresso, sugar, and vanilla extract to make a glossy espresso mixture.
4. Whisk the eggs with the glossy espresso mixture to make an aerated wet batter.
5. Fold the baking soda, cocoa powder, flour, and salt into the wet batter to make the espresso brownie batter.
6. Transfer the batter to the prepared pan and bake at 170 °C for 30–40 minutes, until a tester emerges with moist crumbs but no raw batter.
