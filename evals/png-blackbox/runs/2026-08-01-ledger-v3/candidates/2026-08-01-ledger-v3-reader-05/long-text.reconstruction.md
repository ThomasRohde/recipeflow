# Caramelized Pear and Hazelnut Celebration Tart with Brown-Butter Custard

Makes one 28 cm tart, serving 10–12 people.

## Ingredients

- 180 g unsalted cultured butter, cut into small, equal cubes
- 6 large Conference pears, ripe but still firm, peeled, cored, and cut lengthwise into evenly sized eighths
- 240 mL heavy cream with at least 35% milk fat
- 4 large free-range eggs, at room temperature
- 175 g skinned roasted hazelnuts, with no bitter papery fragments remaining, ground very finely but not processed into nut butter
- 220 g superfine golden caster sugar
- One 28 cm all-butter sweet pastry shell, blind-baked until deeply golden at the edges and completely cooled in its fluted tart pan

## Preparation

1. Position an oven rack in the lower third and preheat the oven, without fan assistance, to 175 °C. Allow at least 20 minutes after the thermostat first signals readiness so the baking stone and oven walls reach an even temperature.
2. Arrange the cooled pastry shell on a rimmed tray beside a heatproof landing area. Use a stable work surface protected from hot caramel and splashing custard.

## Method

1. Brown the butter slowly for 8–12 minutes, until its milk solids are a deep hazelnut brown and it smells nutty, with no black specks. Keep all the toasted milk solids with the fragrant brown butter.
2. In a separate preparation, caramelize every surface of the pear wedges for 12–15 minutes. Each wedge should be amber on both broad sides yet still firm enough to lift intact.
3. Whisk all the brown butter and toasted milk solids with the cream, eggs, finely ground hazelnuts, and sugar. Continue until no dry pockets remain and a ribbon drawn across the surface disappears slowly. The result should be a pourable brown-butter and roasted-hazelnut custard.
4. Arrange all the caramelized pear wedges in the cooled pastry shell, then pour in all the custard. Keep the pear fan visible, allow custard to reach every gap, and do not let it overflow the pastry rim.
5. Carefully transfer the tart to the oven. Bake at 175 °C for 35–45 minutes, until the outer custard is set while the center retains a small, coherent wobble when the tray is nudged.
