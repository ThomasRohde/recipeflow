# Caramelized Pear and Hazelnut Celebration Tart with Brown-Butter Custard

Makes one 28 cm tart, serving 10–12 people.

## Ingredients

- 180 g unsalted cultured butter, cut into small equal cubes
- 6 large, ripe but still firm Conference pears, peeled, cored, and cut lengthwise into evenly sized eighths
- 240 mL heavy cream with at least 35% milk fat
- 4 large free-range eggs, at room temperature
- 175 g skinned roasted hazelnuts, with no bitter papery fragments remaining, ground very finely but not processed into nut butter
- 220 g superfine golden caster sugar
- One 28 cm all-butter sweet pastry shell, blind-baked until deeply golden at the edges and completely cooled in its fluted tart pan

## Preparation

1. Position an oven rack in the lower third and preheat the oven, without fan assistance, to 175 °C. Allow at least 20 minutes after the thermostat first signals readiness so the oven walls—and the baking stone referred to in the source image—reach an even temperature. 2. Arrange the cooled pastry shell on a rimmed tray beside a heatproof landing area. Use a stable work surface protected from hot caramel and splashing custard.

## Method

1. Brown the cubed butter slowly for 8–12 minutes, until the milk solids are a deep hazelnut brown and smell nutty, with no black specks. Set aside the fragrant brown butter with its toasted milk solids.
2. Caramelize every surface of the pear wedges for 12–15 minutes, until each wedge is amber on its two broad sides but remains firm enough to lift intact. The image does not specify a pan, heat setting, or any additional caramelizing ingredient.
3. Whisk together all of the brown butter, cream, eggs, finely ground roasted hazelnuts, and caster sugar. Continue until no dry pockets remain and a ribbon drawn across the surface disappears slowly; the custard should be smooth and pourable.
4. Arrange all of the caramelized pear wedges in the cooled pastry shell so the pear fan remains visible. Pour in all of the custard, making sure it reaches every gap without overflowing the pastry rim.
5. Carefully transfer the tart to the oven and bake at 175 °C for 35–45 minutes, until the outer custard is set but the center retains a small, coherent wobble when the tray is nudged.
