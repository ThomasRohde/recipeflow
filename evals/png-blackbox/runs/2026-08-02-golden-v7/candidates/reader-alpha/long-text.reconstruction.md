# Caramelized Pear and Hazelnut Celebration Tart with Brown-Butter Custard

Yield: one 28 cm tart, serving 10 to 12 people

## Ingredients

- 180 g unsalted cultured butter, cut into small, equal cubes
- 240 mL heavy cream with at least 35% milk fat
- 4 large free-range eggs, at room temperature
- 175 g skinned roasted hazelnuts, with no bitter papery fragments remaining, ground very finely but not into nut butter
- 220 g superfine golden caster sugar
- 6 large, ripe but still firm Conference pears, peeled, cored, and cut lengthwise into evenly sized eighths
- One 28 cm all-butter sweet pastry shell, blind-baked until deeply golden at the edges and completely cooled in its fluted tart pan

## Method

1. Position an oven rack in the lower third and preheat the oven, without fan assistance, to 175 °C. After the thermostat first signals readiness, allow at least 20 more minutes so the baking stone and oven walls reach an even temperature.
2. Arrange the cooled pastry shell on a rimmed tray beside a heatproof landing area—a stable work surface protected from hot caramel and splashing custard.
3. Brown the butter slowly for 8 to 12 minutes, until the milk solids are deep hazelnut brown and smell nutty, with no black specks. The result should be fragrant brown butter with toasted milk solids.
4. Whisk the brown butter with the cream, eggs, ground hazelnuts, and sugar until no dry pockets remain and a ribbon drawn across the surface disappears slowly. This makes a pourable brown-butter and roasted-hazelnut custard.
5. Caramelize every surface of the pear wedges for 12 to 15 minutes. Each wedge should be amber on two broad sides yet tender enough to lift intact. The image does not specify a pan, heat setting, or any additional fat or sugar for this step.
6. Arrange the caramelized pear wedges in the pastry shell and pour in the custard. Keep the fan of pears visible and let the custard reach every gap without overflowing the pastry rim.
7. Bake at 175 °C for 35 to 45 minutes, until the outer custard is set while the center retains a small, coherent wobble when the tray is nudged.
