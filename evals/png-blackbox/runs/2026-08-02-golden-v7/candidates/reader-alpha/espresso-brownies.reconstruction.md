# Espresso Brownies

Yield: 16 brownies

## Ingredients

- 115 g unsalted butter, plus an unspecified amount for the pan
- An unspecified amount of flour for the pan
- 60 mL freshly brewed strong espresso
- 200 g granulated sugar
- 2.5 mL vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs
- 1.3 g baking soda
- 80 g Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 80 g all-purpose flour
- 1.5 g fine sea salt

## Method

1. Butter and flour an 8 × 8 inch baking pan. Preheat the oven to 170 °C.
2. Melt the 115 g unsalted butter.
3. Mix the melted butter with the espresso, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract to make a glossy espresso mixture.
4. Whisk in the eggs to make an aerated wet batter.
5. Fold in the baking soda, cocoa powder, flour, and salt to make the espresso brownie batter.
6. Transfer the batter to the prepared pan and bake at 170 °C for 30 to 40 minutes, until a tester comes out with moist crumbs but no raw batter.
