# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Yield: 4 bowls

## Ingredients

- 4 garlic cloves
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- Salted water for boiling the pasta; the amount of salt and water is not specified

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 210 °C. At the same time, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Roast the garlic cloves and cherry tomatoes for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed. The image does not specify oil, seasoning, a pan, or whether the garlic should be peeled.
3. Crush the roasted garlic and tomatoes into a rustic roasted tomato sauce.
4. Boil the rigatoni for 10 to 12 minutes, until al dente, then drain. The image does not say whether to reserve any pasta water.
5. Tear the basil leaves and toss them with the finely grated Parmesan to make the finishing mixture.
6. Toss the roasted tomato sauce, rigatoni, and basil-Parmesan mixture together until every tube is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed. Divide among four bowls.
