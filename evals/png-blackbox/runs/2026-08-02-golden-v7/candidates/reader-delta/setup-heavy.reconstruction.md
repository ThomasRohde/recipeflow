# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- Finely grated zest and strained juice of 2 lemons
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g softened unsalted butter
- Additional butter and sugar for coating the ramekins; amounts are not specified

## Equipment and advance setup

- Six 180 mL ramekins
- A heavy baking sheet
- A perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk
- A folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C with an empty heavy baking sheet on the center rack.
2. Brush the insides of six 180 mL ramekins upward with additional butter, then coat every interior with additional sugar. This butter and sugar are separate from the measured ingredients above.
3. Chill the perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes.
4. Arrange the folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven to make a landing station.
5. Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved without delay or obstruction.

## Method

1. Cook the lemon zest and strained juice, milk, and egg yolks together to make a thick, warm lemon custard base. The source does not specify the cooking method or time.
2. In the chilled bowl, whip the superfine sugar and egg whites until glossy peaks stand upright but their tips still bend.
3. Gently fold together the warm lemon custard base, the glossy soft-peak meringue, and the 30 g softened butter to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture. The source does not specify their exact order of incorporation.
4. Fill the prepared ramekins evenly, level the mixture, and clean the rims.
5. Transfer the ramekins to the preheated baking sheet and bake at 200 °C for 12 to 14 minutes.
6. Serve the six tall lemon soufflés immediately.
