# Vanilla Custard Base with Two Useful Outputs

Makes one frozen dessert and one pouring sauce; exact yields are not stated.

## Ingredients

- 500 mL heavy cream
- 500 mL whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise
- 180 g granulated sugar
- 8 large egg yolks

## Method

1. Combine the heavy cream, whole milk, and split vanilla bean. Warm and infuse for 20 minutes. The heat level and whether to remove the vanilla bean afterward are not stated.
2. Temper and cook the warm vanilla-infused dairy with the granulated sugar and egg yolks. Heat the custard to 82 °C, until it coats a spoon and a line drawn through it with a finger remains distinct. The exact tempering sequence is not stated.
3. Divide the smooth cooked vanilla custard into two portions; the proportion for each is not stated.
4. Chill one portion to make vanilla crème anglaise for pouring.
5. Churn the other portion to make vanilla bean ice cream. Chilling, churning, and freezing times are not stated.
