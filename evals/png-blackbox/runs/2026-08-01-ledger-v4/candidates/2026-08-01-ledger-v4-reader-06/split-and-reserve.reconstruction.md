# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Makes 6 glasses.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 300 mL cold heavy cream, divided into 250 mL and 50 mL
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Method

1. Melt the finely chopped dark chocolate until smooth. The melting method and temperature are not stated.
2. Divide the cold heavy cream: set aside 250 mL for the mousse base and reserve the remaining 50 mL for the final rosettes.
3. Whip and fold the smooth melted chocolate, the 250 mL cream, and the superfine sugar together until the mixture holds soft peaks and no visible chocolate streaks remain. The precise order and technique for whipping and folding are not stated.
4. Portion the airy dark chocolate mousse among six glasses.
5. Garnish each glass with a rosette made from the reserved cream. No directions are stated for preparing the reserved cream to pipe the rosettes.
