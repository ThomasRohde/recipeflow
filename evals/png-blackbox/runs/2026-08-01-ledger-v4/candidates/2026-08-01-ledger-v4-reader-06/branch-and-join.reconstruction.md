# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Makes 4 bowls.

## Ingredients

- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- 4 garlic cloves
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- Salted water for boiling (quantity and salt amount not stated)

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
2. Boil the rigatoni for 10 to 12 minutes, until al dente. Keep it for the final assembly.
3. Tear the basil leaves and toss them with the finely grated Parmesan to make a finishing mixture. Keep this separate.
4. Roast the garlic cloves and cherry tomatoes in the preheated oven for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed. No oil, seasoning, or further roasting directions are stated.
5. Crush all of the garlic-roasted tomatoes into a rustic roasted tomato sauce. Whether the garlic should be peeled or separately crushed is not stated.
6. Toss together all of the al dente rigatoni, all of the rustic roasted tomato sauce, and all of the basil-Parmesan mixture. Continue until every tube is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed.
7. Divide among four bowls and serve.
