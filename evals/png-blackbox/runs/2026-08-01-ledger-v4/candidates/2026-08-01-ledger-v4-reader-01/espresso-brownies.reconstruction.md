# Espresso Brownies

Makes 16 brownies.

## Ingredients

- 115 g unsalted butter
- 60 mL freshly brewed strong espresso
- 200 g granulated sugar
- 2.5 mL vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs
- 1.3 g baking soda
- 80 g Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 80 g all-purpose flour
- 1.5 g fine sea salt
- Additional butter and flour for the pan; amounts are not stated

## Equipment and preparation

- One 8 × 8 inch baking pan
- Preheat the oven to 170 °C.
- Butter and flour the baking pan.

## Method

1. Melt the 115 g unsalted butter.
2. Mix the freshly brewed strong espresso, all of the melted butter, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract into a glossy espresso mixture.
3. Whisk the eggs into the glossy espresso mixture to make an aerated wet batter.
4. Fold the baking soda, Dutch-process cocoa powder, all-purpose flour, and fine sea salt into the wet batter to make the espresso brownie batter.
5. Transfer the batter to the prepared pan. Bake at 170 °C for 30 to 40 minutes, until a tester emerges with moist crumbs but no raw batter.
