# Vanilla Custard Base with Two Useful Outputs

Makes one frozen dessert and one pouring sauce; the division between them is not specified.

## Ingredients

- 500 mL heavy cream
- 500 mL whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise
- 180 g granulated sugar
- 8 large egg yolks

## Method

1. Warm the heavy cream, whole milk, and split vanilla bean. Infuse for 20 minutes to make warm vanilla-infused dairy.
2. Temper and cook the granulated sugar, all of the warm vanilla-infused dairy, and the egg yolks. Heat to 82 °C and continue until the custard coats a spoon and a line drawn through it with a finger remains distinct.
3. Divide the smooth cooked vanilla custard into two portions. No proportion is specified.
4. Chill one portion to make crème anglaise for pouring.
5. Churn the other portion to make vanilla bean ice cream.

Chilling and churning times are not specified.
