# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Makes 4 bowls.

## Ingredients

- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- 4 garlic cloves
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- A large pot of salted water; the amount of salt is not specified

## Method

1. Bring the large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
3. Boil the rigatoni for 10–12 minutes, until al dente.
4. Tear the basil leaves and toss them with the finely grated Parmesan to make a finishing mixture. Keep it separate.
5. Roast the garlic cloves and cherry tomatoes in the preheated oven for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes and garlic are collapsed.
6. Crush all of the roasted garlic and tomatoes into a rustic roasted tomato sauce.
7. Toss all of the al dente rigatoni, all of the rustic roasted tomato sauce, and all of the basil-Parmesan mixture together until every tube of pasta is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed.
8. Divide into four bowls.
