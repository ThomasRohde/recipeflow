# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Makes 6 glasses.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 300 mL cold heavy cream, divided into 250 mL and 50 mL
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Method

1. Melt the finely chopped dark chocolate until smooth.
2. Divide the cold heavy cream: use 250 mL for the mousse base and hold back 50 mL for the final rosettes.
3. Whip and fold together the smooth melted chocolate, all 250 mL of cream for the mousse base, and the superfine sugar. Continue until the mixture holds soft peaks and has no visible chocolate streaks.
4. Portion all of the airy dark chocolate mousse among six glasses.
5. Use all of the reserved 50 mL cream to finish the glasses with cream rosettes.
