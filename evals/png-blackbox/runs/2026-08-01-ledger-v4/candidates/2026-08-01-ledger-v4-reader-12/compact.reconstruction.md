# Cinnamon Toast

Makes 2 slices.

## Ingredients

- 2 slices sourdough bread
- 2 tablespoons cinnamon butter

## Method

1. Spread the cinnamon butter over the two slices of sourdough bread.
2. Toast for 3 to 5 minutes to make two slices of cinnamon toast.
