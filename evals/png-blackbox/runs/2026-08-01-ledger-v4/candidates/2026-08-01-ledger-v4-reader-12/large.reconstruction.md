# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- Zest and juice of 3 limes
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided into 30 mL and 45 mL portions
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds
- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt

## Preparation

- Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat for the sauce.
- Preheat a wide, heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately.
- Rinse the basmati rice until the water is clear, soak it for 30 minutes, and drain it thoroughly.
- Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls.

## Method

1. Combine the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
2. Blend the cilantro with the lime zest and juice until the sauce is vivid green and pourable but still has tiny visible herb flecks. Set it aside.
3. Put the thoroughly drained rice and 900 mL filtered water over low heat. Steam for 14 to 18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom.
4. In the heavy pot over low heat, sauté the onion, garlic, and ginger mixture in 30 mL of the oil for 10 to 12 minutes, until it becomes a soft, golden aromatic base.
5. Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes to the aromatic base. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind a spoon.
6. In the preheated skillet, toast the sliced almonds for 3 to 5 minutes, until evenly golden. Set them aside.
7. In the preheated skillet, toast the coriander and cumin seeds for 2 to 3 minutes, until fragrant.
8. Combine the chicken pieces, salt, all of the toasted spices, and yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes. The image does not specify a storage temperature during marination.
9. Using the remaining 45 mL oil in the wide skillet over medium heat, brown the marinated chicken in batches for 12 to 16 minutes, until the pieces are deeply browned.
10. Combine the browned chicken with the tomato-aromatic sauce. Braise over low heat for 25 to 35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.
11. On the warmed platter, spread the rice in an even base. Arrange the chicken so the pieces remain distinct, distribute the cilantro-lime sauce in ribbons, and finish with the toasted almonds so they stay crisp on top.
12. Serve in the warmed shallow bowls.
