# Crème brûlée au café

Makes 6 ramekins.

## Ingredients

- 60 mL freshly prepared espresso
- 600 mL 35% heavy cream
- 1/2 Madagascar vanilla pod
- 90 g caster sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- 36 g sugar for caramelizing

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 150°C. Prepare a deep dish of hot water for a bain-marie; the water should come halfway up the ramekins.
2. Combine the espresso, cream, and vanilla pod. Keep the mixture warm and let it infuse for 15 minutes.
3. Add the caster sugar and egg yolks to the infused cream mixture. Whisk gently until homogeneous, avoiding a thick layer of foam on the surface.
4. Divide the custard mixture among six ramekins. Set them in the prepared bain-marie and bake at 150°C for 35 to 45 minutes, until the edges are set while the centers still tremble like a soft jelly.
