# Vanilla Custard Base with Two Useful Outputs

Produces one frozen dessert and one pouring sauce; the division proportions and final quantities are not specified.

## Ingredients

- 500 mL heavy cream
- 500 mL whole milk
- 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise
- 180 g granulated sugar
- 8 large egg yolks

## Method

1. Combine the heavy cream, whole milk, and split vanilla bean. Keep the mixture warm and infuse it for 20 minutes.
2. Temper and cook the warm vanilla-infused dairy with the granulated sugar and egg yolks, heating the custard to 82 °C. Continue until it coats a spoon and a line drawn through it with a finger remains distinct.
3. Divide the smooth cooked vanilla custard into two portions. The image does not specify how much should go into each portion.
4. Chill one portion to make vanilla crème anglaise for pouring. No chilling time or temperature is specified.
5. Churn the other portion to make vanilla bean ice cream. No churning or freezing conditions are specified.
