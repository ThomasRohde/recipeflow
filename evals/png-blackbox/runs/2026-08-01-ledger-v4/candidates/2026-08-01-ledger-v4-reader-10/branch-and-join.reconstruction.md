# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Makes 4 bowls.

## Ingredients

- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- Garlic cloves: the image shows a quantity of `4` but also the text `4 g garlic cloves`; the intended amount is unclear
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- A large pot of salted water

## Method

1. Bring the large pot of salted water to a boil. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
2. Boil the rigatoni in the salted water for 10–12 minutes, until al dente.
3. Tear the basil leaves and toss them with the finely grated Parmesan to make a finishing mixture. Keep this separate.
4. Roast the garlic and cherry tomatoes in the preheated oven for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed.
5. Crush all of the roasted garlic and tomatoes into a rustic roasted tomato sauce.
6. Toss together all of the al dente rigatoni, all of the roasted tomato sauce, and all of the basil-Parmesan mixture until every tube is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed.
7. Divide among four bowls.
