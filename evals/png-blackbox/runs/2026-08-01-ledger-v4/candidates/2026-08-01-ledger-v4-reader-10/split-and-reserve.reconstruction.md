# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Makes 6 glasses.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 300 mL cold heavy cream
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Method

1. Melt the finely chopped dark chocolate until smooth.
2. Divide the cold heavy cream into 250 mL for the mousse base and 50 mL to reserve for the final rosettes. Keep the reserved 50 mL separate.
3. Whip and fold together all of the melted chocolate, the 250 mL cream, and the superfine sugar until the mixture holds soft peaks and has no visible chocolate streaks.
4. Portion all of the airy chocolate mousse among six glasses.
5. Garnish the glasses with rosettes made from all of the reserved cream.
