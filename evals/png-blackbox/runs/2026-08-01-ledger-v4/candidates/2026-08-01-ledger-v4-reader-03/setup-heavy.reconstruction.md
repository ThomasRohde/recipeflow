# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- Finely grated zest and strained juice of 2 lemons
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g softened unsalted butter
- Additional butter, amount unspecified, for preparing the ramekins
- Additional sugar, amount unspecified, for coating the ramekins

## Before you begin

- Make sure a mixing bowl and balloon whisk are perfectly clean, then chill them for 15 minutes.
- Clear the approach to the oven so filled ramekins can be moved without delay or obstruction.
- Set a folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven.
- Put an empty heavy baking sheet on the center oven rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.
- Brush the insides of six 180 mL ramekins upward with butter, then coat every interior with sugar.

## Method

1. Cook the lemon zest and juice, milk, and egg yolks together until they form a thick, warm lemon custard base. The image gives no cooking time, temperature, or more specific method for this stage.
2. In the chilled bowl, whip the egg whites with the superfine sugar until the meringue is glossy and its peaks stand upright while their tips still bend.
3. Gently fold the softened butter, all of the warm lemon custard base, and all of the meringue together to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture.
4. Divide the mixture evenly among the prepared ramekins, level the tops, and clean the rims.
5. Transfer the filled ramekins to the preheated heavy baking sheet. Bake at 200 °C for 12 to 14 minutes, until the soufflés are tall. Serve immediately.
