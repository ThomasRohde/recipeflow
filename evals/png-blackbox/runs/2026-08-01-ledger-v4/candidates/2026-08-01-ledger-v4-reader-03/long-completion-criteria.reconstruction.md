# Slow Caramel with Explicit Completion Criteria

Makes 450 mL sauce.

## Ingredients

- 300 g granulated sugar
- 90 mL filtered water
- 60 g unsalted butter, cut into 6 pieces
- 240 mL warm heavy cream
- 4 g flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Combine the sugar and filtered water and heat over medium heat without stirring for 4 to 6 minutes. Continue until every visible sugar crystal has disappeared from both the syrup and the inner wall of the pan, and the liquid is completely transparent when viewed against a white spatula.
2. Keep the syrup over medium heat for 8 to 12 minutes, swirling occasionally. Stop when it is evenly deep amber from edge to center, gives off its first faint thread of smoke, and has no dark, bitter spots.
3. Reduce the heat to low. Carefully whisk in the six pieces of butter, the warm heavy cream, and the flaky sea salt. Continue for 3 to 5 minutes, until the bubbling has subsided, every piece of butter is incorporated, and a spoonful cooled for testing falls in a thick ribbon and remains smooth rather than grainy.
