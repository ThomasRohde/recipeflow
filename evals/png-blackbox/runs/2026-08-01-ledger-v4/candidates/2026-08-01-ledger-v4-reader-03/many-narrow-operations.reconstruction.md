# Hand-Folded Dumplings Through Many Narrow Operations

Makes 24 dumplings.

## Ingredients

- 480 g rested dumpling dough
- 360 g chilled cabbage and mushroom filling
- 3 L water, divided: 30 mL for sealing and 2,970 mL for boiling
- 15 mL toasted sesame oil

## Method

1. Divide the rested dough into 24 equal portions.
2. Round all the portions into smooth dough balls.
3. Flatten all the balls into thick dough discs.
4. Roll all the discs into thin, round wrappers.
5. Divide the chilled cabbage and mushroom filling among the wrappers, leaving them open.
6. Use the reserved 30 mL water to moisten the wrapper rims.
7. Fold the wrappers into half-moons.
8. Pleat and seal them tightly.
9. Bring the remaining 2,970 mL water to a boil. Boil the dumplings for 5 to 7 minutes, until they are cooked and floating.
10. Drain the dumplings thoroughly.
11. Toss all the drained dumplings with the toasted sesame oil until coated.
12. Serve the 24 hand-folded mushroom dumplings.
