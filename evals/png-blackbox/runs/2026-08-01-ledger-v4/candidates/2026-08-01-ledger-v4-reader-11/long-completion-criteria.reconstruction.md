# Slow Caramel with Explicit Completion Criteria

Makes 450 mL sauce

## Ingredients

- 300 g granulated sugar
- 90 mL filtered water
- 60 g unsalted butter, cut into 6 pieces
- 240 mL warm heavy cream
- 4 g flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Combine the granulated sugar and filtered water. Heat over medium heat for 4 to 6 minutes without stirring, until every visible crystal has disappeared from both the syrup and the inner wall of the pan and the liquid looks completely transparent against a white spatula.
2. Continue cooking over medium heat for 8 to 12 minutes, swirling occasionally, until the syrup is evenly deep amber from edge to center, gives off its first faint thread of smoke, and has no dark, bitter spots.
3. Reduce the heat to low. Carefully whisk in the six pieces of butter, the warm heavy cream, and the flaky sea salt. Continue for 3 to 5 minutes, until the bubbling has subsided, every piece of butter is incorporated, and a cooled spoonful forms a thick ribbon that stays smooth rather than grainy.

The finished sauce should be smooth and salted, with a deep amber finish.
