# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés

## Ingredients

- 2 lemons, finely zested and juiced; strain the juice
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g unsalted butter, softened
- Additional butter and sugar for coating the ramekins; quantities are not shown

## Equipment and advance setup

- Six 180 mL ramekins
- A perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk
- A heavy baking sheet
- A folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer

1. Chill the clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes.
2. Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved there without delay or obstruction.
3. Arrange the folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven.
4. Put the empty heavy baking sheet on the center oven rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.
5. Brush the six ramekins upward with butter, then coat every interior with sugar.

## Method

1. Cook the finely grated zest and strained juice of the 2 lemons with the whole milk and egg yolks until they form a thick, warm lemon custard base. The heat, cooking time, and order of combination are not shown.
2. Using the chilled bowl and whisk, whip the egg whites with the superfine sugar to a glossy soft-peak meringue. Stop when the glossy peaks stand upright but their tips still bend.
3. Gently fold together the softened unsalted butter, all of the warm lemon custard base, and all of the meringue to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture. The exact folding sequence is not shown.
4. Using the prepared equipment beside the oven, fill the prepared ramekins evenly. Level them and clean their rims.
5. Immediately transfer the ramekins to the preheated heavy baking sheet. Bake at 200 °C for 12 to 14 minutes.
6. Serve the six tall lemon soufflés immediately.
