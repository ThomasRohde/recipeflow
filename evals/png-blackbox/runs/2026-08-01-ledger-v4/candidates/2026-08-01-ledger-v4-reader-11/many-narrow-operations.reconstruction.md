# Hand-Folded Dumplings Through Many Narrow Operations

Makes 24 dumplings

This recipe begins with prepared, rested dumpling dough and prepared, chilled cabbage-and-mushroom filling. Their preparation is not shown.

## Ingredients

- 480 g rested dumpling dough
- 360 g chilled cabbage-and-mushroom filling
- 3 L water for sealing and boiling, divided into 30 mL for sealing and 2,970 mL for boiling
- 15 mL toasted sesame oil

## Method

1. Divide the rested dumpling dough into 24 equal portions.
2. Round all the portions into smooth dough balls.
3. Flatten all the dough balls into thick discs.
4. Roll all the thick discs into thin, round wrappers.
5. Divide all the chilled filling among the wrappers, leaving them open.
6. Use the allocated 30 mL water to moisten the rims of the filled wrappers.
7. Fold the wrappers into half-moons.
8. Pleat them into tightly sealed dumplings.
9. Boil the dumplings in the remaining 2,970 mL water for 5 to 7 minutes, until they are cooked and floating.
10. Drain the dumplings thoroughly.
11. Toss all the drained dumplings with the toasted sesame oil until coated.
12. Serve the 24 hand-folded mushroom dumplings.
