# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- 3 limes, zest and juice
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided into 30 mL and 45 mL
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds
- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt

## Preparation

- Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat for sautéing the aromatics.
- Over medium heat, preheat a wide, heavy skillet until a drop of water evaporates immediately. This skillet is used for the almonds, spices, and chicken.
- Rinse the basmati rice until the water runs clear, then soak it for 30 minutes.
- Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls for serving.

## Method

1. Combine the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
2. Blend the cilantro with the zest and juice of the three limes until the sauce is vivid green and pourable while retaining tiny visible herb flecks. Set this separate sauce aside.
3. Drain the soaked basmati rice thoroughly.
4. Combine the drained rice with 900 mL filtered water and steam over low heat for 14–18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom. Set the rice aside.
5. In the warm heavy lidded pot over low heat, sauté the onion, garlic, and ginger mixture in 30 mL of the neutral oil for 10–12 minutes, until soft and golden.
6. Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes to the aromatic base. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind a spoon. Set the concentrated tomato and aromatic sauce aside.
7. In the preheated skillet over medium heat, toast the sliced almonds for 3–5 minutes, until evenly golden. Set them aside.
8. In the same preheated skillet over medium heat, toast the coriander and cumin seeds for 2–3 minutes, until fragrant. Set the toasted spices aside.
9. Combine the chicken thigh pieces with the salt, all of the toasted cumin and coriander, and the yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes.
10. Using the remaining 45 mL neutral oil in the preheated skillet over medium heat, brown the marinated chicken in batches for 12–16 minutes, until deeply browned.
11. Combine all of the browned chicken with all of the concentrated tomato and aromatic sauce. Braise over low heat for 25–35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to every piece.
12. On the warmed platter, make an even base of all the steamed rice. Keep the chicken pieces distinct as you arrange all the chicken braise over it. Distribute all the herb sauce in ribbons, then top with all the toasted almonds so they remain crisp. Serve with the eight warmed shallow bowls.
