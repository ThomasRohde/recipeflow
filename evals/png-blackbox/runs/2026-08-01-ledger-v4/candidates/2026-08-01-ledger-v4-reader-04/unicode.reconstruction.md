# Crème brûlée au café

Makes 6 ramekins.

## Ingredients

- 60 mL freshly prepared espresso
- 600 mL heavy cream with 35% fat
- 1/2 Madagascar vanilla bean
- 90 g granulated sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- 36 g sugar for caramelizing

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 150 °C. Prepare a hot water bath in a deep dish, using enough hot water to come halfway up the ramekins.
2. Warm the espresso, cream, and vanilla together for 15 minutes to make a coffee-and-vanilla-infused cream.
3. Gently whisk all of the infused cream with the granulated sugar and egg yolks until the mixture is homogeneous, without a thick layer of foam on the surface.
4. Divide the mixture among six ramekins and place them in the prepared water bath. The image implies this division into ramekins but does not show it as a separate instruction.
5. Bake at 150 °C for 35–45 minutes, until the edges are set but the centers still tremble like a soft jelly.
6. Cool the six custards. No cooling time or method is specified.
7. Distribute the 36 g caramelizing sugar over the cooled custards and caramelize it until each ramekin has a thin, amber, crisp crust with no visible white sugar. The caramelization tool or heat source is not specified.
