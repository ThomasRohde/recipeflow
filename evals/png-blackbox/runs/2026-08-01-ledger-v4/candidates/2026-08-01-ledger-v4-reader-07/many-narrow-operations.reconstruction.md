# Hand-Folded Dumplings Through Many Narrow Operations

Makes 24 dumplings.

## Ingredients

- 480 g rested dumpling dough
- 360 g chilled cabbage and mushroom filling
- 3 L water, divided into 30 mL for sealing and 2,970 mL for boiling
- 15 mL toasted sesame oil

The image treats the rested dough and chilled filling as already prepared and does not provide their recipes.

## Method

1. Divide the rested dumpling dough into 24 equal portions.
2. Round all the portions into smooth dough balls.
3. Flatten all the balls into thick dough discs.
4. Roll all the discs into thin round wrappers. No target diameter or thickness is shown.
5. Divide the chilled cabbage and mushroom filling among the wrappers, leaving them open.
6. Use 30 mL of the water to moisten the rims of all the filled wrappers.
7. Fold the wrappers into half-moons.
8. Pleat them so the dumplings are tightly sealed.
9. Boil the dumplings in the remaining 2,970 mL water for 5 to 7 minutes, until they are cooked and floating.
10. Drain the dumplings thoroughly.
11. Toss the drained dumplings with the toasted sesame oil until coated.
12. Serve all 24 hand-folded mushroom dumplings.
