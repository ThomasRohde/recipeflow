# Slow Caramel with Explicit Completion Criteria

Makes 450 mL sauce.

## Ingredients

- 300 g granulated sugar
- 90 mL filtered water
- 60 g unsalted butter, cut into 6 pieces
- 240 mL warm heavy cream
- 4 g flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Combine the sugar and filtered water over medium heat and dissolve the sugar without stirring, allowing 4 to 6 minutes. Continue until every visible crystal has disappeared from both the syrup and the inner wall of the pan and the liquid looks completely transparent against a white spatula.
2. Keep the clear concentrated syrup over medium heat and caramelize it, swirling occasionally, for 8 to 12 minutes. Stop when the syrup is an even deep amber from edge to center and gives off its first faint thread of smoke, before any dark bitter spots develop.
3. Reduce the heat to low. Carefully whisk in the six pieces of butter, the warm heavy cream, and the flaky sea salt over 3 to 5 minutes. Continue until the bubbling has subsided, every piece of butter is incorporated, and a spoonful cooled for checking forms a thick ribbon that remains smooth rather than grainy.

The finished sauce should be smooth and salted, with a deep amber finish.
