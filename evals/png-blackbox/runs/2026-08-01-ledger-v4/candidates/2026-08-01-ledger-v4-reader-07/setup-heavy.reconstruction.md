# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- Finely grated zest and strained juice of 2 lemons
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites
- 30 g softened unsalted butter
- Butter and sugar for coating the ramekins; amounts are not shown

## Set up

- Chill a perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes before whipping the egg whites.
- Clear the approach to the oven so the filled ramekins can be moved without delay or obstruction.
- Place a folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven.
- Put an empty heavy baking sheet on the center oven rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.
- Brush the insides of six 180 mL ramekins upward with butter, then coat every interior with sugar.

## Method

1. Cook the lemon zest and strained juice, whole milk, and egg yolks to make a thick, warm lemon custard base. The image does not provide a time, heat setting, or more detailed technique for this step.
2. In the chilled bowl, whip the superfine sugar and egg whites until the meringue is glossy and its peaks stand upright while their tips still bend.
3. Gently fold together all of the warm lemon custard base, all of the glossy soft-peak meringue, and the 30 g softened butter to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture. The image does not specify an order for combining these three components.
4. Divide the mixture evenly among the prepared ramekins, level the tops, and clean the rims.
5. Transfer the ramekins to the preheated heavy baking sheet and bake at 200 °C for 12 to 14 minutes, producing six tall lemon soufflés. Serve immediately.
