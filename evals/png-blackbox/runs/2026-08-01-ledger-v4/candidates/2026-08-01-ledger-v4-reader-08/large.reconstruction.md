# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- 3 limes, zest and juice
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided into 30 mL and 45 mL
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds
- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 300 g thick plain yogurt

## Preparation

- Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat for the sauce.
- Preheat a wide heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately.
- Rinse the basmati rice until the water is clear, soak it for 30 minutes, and drain it thoroughly.
- Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls.

## Method

1. Combine the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions.
2. Blend the cilantro with the zest and juice of the three limes until the sauce is vivid green and pourable while still retaining tiny visible herb flecks. Keep this sauce separate.
3. Put the thoroughly drained rice and 900 mL filtered water together and steam over low heat for 14 to 18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom. Keep the rice separate.
4. In the warm heavy lidded pot over low heat, sauté all of the garlic, ginger, and onion mixture in 30 mL of the neutral oil for 10 to 12 minutes, producing a soft, golden aromatic base.
5. Add the chicken stock and peeled plum tomatoes to the aromatic base. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and the sauce leaves a brief trail behind the spoon.
6. In the preheated wide skillet over medium heat, toast the sliced almonds for 3 to 5 minutes, until evenly golden. Keep them separate.
7. In the preheated wide skillet over medium heat, toast the coriander and cumin seeds for 2 to 3 minutes, until fragrant.
8. Combine the chicken thigh pieces, salt, all of the toasted coriander and cumin, and yogurt. Marinate for 30 minutes.
9. Using the preheated wide skillet over medium heat, brown all of the marinated chicken in batches in the remaining 45 mL neutral oil for 12 to 16 minutes, producing deeply browned chicken pieces.
10. Combine all of the browned chicken with the tomato and aromatic sauce. Braise over low heat for 25 to 35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.
11. On the warm platter, make an even base of all the rice. Layer on all the chicken braise while keeping the chicken distinct, distribute all the herb sauce in ribbons, and top with all the almonds so they remain crisp. Serve with the eight warm shallow bowls.
