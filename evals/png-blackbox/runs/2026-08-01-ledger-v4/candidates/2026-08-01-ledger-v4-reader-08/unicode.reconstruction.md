# Crème brûlée au café · Καραμέλα · コーヒー

Makes 6 ramekins.

## Ingredients

- 60 mL freshly brewed espresso coffee
- 600 mL 35% whole cream
- 1/2 Madagascar vanilla bean
- 90 g granulated sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- 36 g sugar for caramelizing

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 150 °C. Set the six ramekins in a deep dish and prepare a hot-water bath that will reach halfway up their sides.
2. Combine the espresso, cream, and vanilla bean. Keep the mixture warm for 15 minutes to infuse it.
3. Gently whisk all of the infused cream mixture with the granulated sugar and egg yolks until homogeneous, without a thick layer of foam on the surface.
4. Divide the mixture among the six ramekins. Bake them in the hot-water bath at 150 °C for 35 to 45 minutes, until the edges are set while the centres still wobble like a soft jelly. Cool the six custards; no cooling time or temperature is specified.
5. Distribute the caramelizing sugar over the cooled custards and caramelize it until every ramekin has a thin, amber, crisp crust with no white sugar visible. The caramelizing method is not specified.
