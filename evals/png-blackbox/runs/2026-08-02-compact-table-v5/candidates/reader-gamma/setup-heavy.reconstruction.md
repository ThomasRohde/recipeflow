# Setup-Heavy Lemon Soufflé

Makes 6 individual soufflés.

## Ingredients

- 30 g softened unsalted butter
- Finely grated zest and strained juice of 2 lemons
- 250 mL whole milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 120 g superfine sugar
- 6 egg whites

## Equipment and setup

- Six 180 mL ramekins
- Heavy baking sheet
- Perfectly clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk
- Folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer

1. Put an empty heavy baking sheet on the center oven rack and preheat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Brush the ramekins upward with softened butter, then coat every interior with sugar. The image does not specify how much of the listed butter and sugar to reserve for this preparation.
3. Chill the clean mixing bowl and balloon whisk for 15 minutes.
4. Arrange the folded towel, ladle, offset spatula, and timer beside the oven, and clear the oven approach so the filled ramekins can be transferred without delay or obstruction.

## Method

1. Cook the lemon zest and strained juice with the milk and egg yolks until they form a thick, warm lemon custard base. No cooking time or temperature is shown.
2. In the chilled bowl, whip the egg whites with the superfine sugar until glossy peaks stand upright but their tips still bend.
3. Fold gently to make an aerated lemon soufflé mixture. The image associates the 30 g softened butter with this folding stage as well as with preparing the ramekins, but does not make the division clear.
4. Fill the six ramekins evenly and level the tops; clean the rims.
5. Transfer the ramekins to the preheated baking sheet and bake at 200 °C for 12 to 14 minutes.
6. Serve the six tall lemon soufflés immediately.
