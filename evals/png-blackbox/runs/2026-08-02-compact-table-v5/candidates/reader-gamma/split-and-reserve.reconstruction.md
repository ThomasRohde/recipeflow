# Chocolate Mousse with Reserved Cream

Makes 6 glasses.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 300 mL cold heavy cream, divided
- 30 g superfine sugar

## Method

1. Melt the chopped dark chocolate until smooth.
2. Divide the cold cream into 250 mL for the mousse and 50 mL to reserve for the final rosettes.
3. Whip the mousse portion of cream with the superfine sugar, then fold it together with the melted chocolate. Continue only until the mixture holds soft peaks and no visible chocolate streaks remain.
4. Portion the airy dark chocolate mousse among six glasses.
5. Use the reserved cream to garnish each glass with a cream rosette. The image does not state whether this reserved portion should be whipped separately, so prepare it only as needed to form the rosettes.
