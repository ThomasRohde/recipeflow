# Layered Spiced Chicken, Rice, Herb Sauce, and Toasted Almond Platter

Makes 8 generous servings.

## Ingredients

- 1.6 kg boneless chicken thigh pieces
- 18 g fine sea salt
- 2 teaspoons whole coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons whole cumin seeds
- 300 g thick plain yogurt
- 75 mL neutral cooking oil, divided
- 8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 50 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 500 mL unsalted chicken stock
- 800 g peeled plum tomatoes
- 80 g cilantro leaves and tender stems
- Zest and juice of 3 limes
- 600 g aged basmati rice
- 900 mL filtered water
- 120 g blanched sliced almonds

## Preparation

1. Heat a wide, heavy skillet over medium heat until a drop of water evaporates immediately. It will be used for the spices, chicken, and almonds.
2. Warm a heavy lidded pot over low heat for the sauce.
3. Rinse the basmati rice until the water runs clear, soak it for 30 minutes, then drain it thoroughly.
4. Warm a broad serving platter and eight shallow bowls.

## Method

1. Toast the coriander and cumin seeds in the hot skillet for 2 to 3 minutes, until fragrant.
2. Marinate the chicken with the toasted spices, sea salt, and yogurt for 30 minutes.
3. Heat 45 mL of the neutral oil in the skillet and brown the chicken in batches for 12 to 16 minutes, until the pieces are deeply browned. Keep them for the braise.
4. Combine the chopped garlic, grated ginger, and sliced onions. Heat the remaining 30 mL oil in the heavy pot and sauté these aromatics for 10 to 12 minutes, until soft and golden.
5. Add the chicken stock and peeled tomatoes. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the tomatoes have broken down and a spoon drawn through the sauce leaves a brief trail.
6. Add the browned chicken to the sauce. Braise over low heat for 25 to 35 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce clings to each piece.
7. Blend the cilantro leaves and tender stems with the lime zest and juice until the sauce is vivid green and pourable, with tiny visible herb flecks. The image gives no additional liquid or seasoning for this sauce.
8. Put the drained rice and 900 mL filtered water into a pot. Steam over low heat for 14 to 18 minutes, until every grain is tender and separate and no water remains at the bottom.
9. Toast the sliced almonds in the skillet for 3 to 5 minutes, until evenly golden.
10. Spread the rice in an even layer on the warm platter. Arrange the chicken so it remains distinct, distribute the herb sauce in ribbons, and finish with the almonds on top so they stay crisp.
11. Serve in the eight warmed shallow bowls.
