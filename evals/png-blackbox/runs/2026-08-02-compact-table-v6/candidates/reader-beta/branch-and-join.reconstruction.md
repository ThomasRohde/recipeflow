# Roasted Tomato Pasta with Three Converging Branches

Makes 4 bowls.

## Ingredients

- 30 g fresh basil leaves
- 80 g finely grated Parmesan
- 400 g dried rigatoni
- 4 garlic cloves
- 600 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- Salt for the pasta water; the image does not give an amount

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
3. Tear the basil leaves and toss them with the grated Parmesan to make the finishing mixture.
4. Boil the rigatoni for 10 to 12 minutes, until al dente, then drain. The image does not mention reserving pasta water.
5. Roast the garlic cloves and cherry tomatoes for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes have collapsed.
6. Crush the roasted garlic and tomatoes together into a rustic roasted tomato sauce.
7. Toss the al dente rigatoni with the roasted tomato sauce and the basil-Parmesan finishing mixture until every tube is coated and the finishing mixture is evenly distributed.
8. Divide among four bowls.
