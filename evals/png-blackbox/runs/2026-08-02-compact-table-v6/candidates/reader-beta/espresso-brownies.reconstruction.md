# Espresso Brownies

Makes 16 brownies.

## Ingredients

- 1.3 g baking soda
- 80 g Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 80 g all-purpose flour
- 1.5 g fine sea salt
- 2 large eggs
- 60 mL freshly brewed strong espresso
- 115 g unsalted butter
- 200 g granulated sugar
- 2.5 mL vanilla extract
- Additional butter and flour for preparing the pan; the image does not give amounts

## Method

1. Butter and flour an 8 × 8 inch baking pan.
2. Preheat the oven to 170 °C.
3. Melt the unsalted butter.
4. Mix the freshly brewed espresso, granulated sugar, vanilla extract, and melted butter until glossy.
5. Whisk in the eggs to make an aerated wet batter. The image does not specify whether to add them one at a time.
6. Fold in the baking soda, cocoa powder, flour, and salt to make the espresso brownie batter.
7. Transfer the batter to the prepared pan and bake at 170 °C for 30 to 40 minutes, until a tester emerges with moist crumbs but no raw batter.
8. Portion into 16 fudgy espresso brownies. The image does not specify a cooling time before cutting.
